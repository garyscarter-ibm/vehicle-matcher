3. What removing Katie's flagged questions would do

Remove "Where will it live?" (primaryUse)

This question is the only direct signal for: urban cruiser family (use-case)

It also feeds the boot need calculation (now that the boot question was removed — primaryUse + people together derive it). Removing it means the engine loses half of that derived signal and boot-space matching degrades.

Risk: Medium. Urban vs long-distance is a real differentiator (e.g. hatchback vs estate/SUV). But Katie's right that the option labels are quite similar for MINI — "nipping round town" vs "daily dash" is fuzzy. Could be fixed with better copy rather than removal.

Remove "Who's along for the ride?" (people)

Together with primaryUse, this is what replaced the boot question. It's also the only signal for seat count.

Cars with 7 seats (X7) or 4 seats only (M2, Z4, 840i) only get correctly filtered via this question.

Risk: High if both this AND primaryUse are removed — boot need becomes uncomputable. Lower risk if only one is removed.

Remove "Pick your top two priorities"

This is the only direct booster for: tech image economy

Without it, tech-focused cars (iX, i5) and image-focused cars (8 Series, XM) only surface if other answers happen to align. The style (comfort/sporty) question partially covers "performance" but not the others.

Risk: Medium. Katie's concern is valid — it can feel like a repeat. But it's the only place a user says "I care about tech" directly.

Katie's proposal: add trim tier question

"Classic / Exclusive / Sport" — not in the engine yet.

The engine currently has no trim-tier signal. Adding this could map to: Classic → efficientpractical, Exclusive → imagetech, Sport → drivers-carperformance.

Opportunity: Would need a new scoresAs mapping in brands.js. Katie's social desirability concern is worth noting — "Exclusive" may deter people who'd actually love a Countryman Exclusive.
