underlying fit using the shared scorePreferences/rankCars helper on the same already
checked snapshots, then retain both scores and full reasons. Reuse this immutable matrix
in future route consumers. Null fit or missing scored evidence cannot pass threshold.
The real66 mandatory claim/review/allocate revalidations remain mandatory and are counted
separately from shared seed evaluation; caching cannot bypass current claim authority.

once-per-seed matrix cannot blindly cache future availability as permanently unsuitable.

at the fixed comparison origin; retain actual release dates separately and apply them
through the real66 runtime stock projection and clock at claim time. Required-by,
underlying needs, canonical policy/points and unknown facts remain real policy checks.
This projection contract needs root review before implementation; no cached timing gate
may be presented as actual release/claim evidence.

stable tie-breaks. Snapshot a simultaneous access batch before executing it serially, so
later contenders can produce real66 refusal rather than a synthetic conflict counter.

cached appropriate candidate, count the refused attempt once. Do not treat unavailable,
invalid or policy errors as ordinary contention. Respond only before renewal/expiry;
unacceptable/no-response holds remain untaken until the real expiry handler frees them.
Read real hold expiry; no second expiry engine. No retry-induced hold renewal. The keyed
manual fallback is shared with future buddy nonparticipants, whose route is out of scope.
