# P01: Employee car chooser

**Status:** Provisional v0.1, 31 October 2026. Discovery input for FUCQ-2.

A BMW Group employee approaching their next scheme-car decision. The work-context anchor is a BMW Financial Services development manager and product owner. Scheme participation is an assumed scenario, not a known fact about the source contact. [E1](sources.md#e1-employee-role)

**Job statement — authored summary:** When I need my next scheme car, help me choose something that meets my needs, fits my entitlement and contribution limit, and is available when I need it, so I can feel confident about my choice.

## Context supported by evidence

- The source contact works for BMW Financial Services and holds a development/product-owner role. The corpus provides no evidence of their household, commute, grade, current vehicle or scheme preferences. [E1](sources.md#e1-employee-role)
- The scheme involves grade/model eligibility, driver restrictions, monthly points and potentially cash contributions. The accepted FS/UK baseline differs from the RR/Production matrix; a job title does not establish a grade. [E8](sources.md#e8-scheme-rules-and-eligibility)
- The documented selection journey is constrained by vehicles already ordered or in stock. Selection, approval and handover are separate stages. A visible car is not a guaranteed allocation. [E7](sources.md#e7-portal-walkthrough)
- Earlier discovery describes time pressure and dissatisfaction with the choice available. Historical selections may record compromises rather than what employees wanted. This is a research finding, not evidence that this particular contact was dissatisfied. [E6](sources.md#e6-scheme-operations-research) [E9](sources.md#e9-earlier-proposals)

## Motivations and behaviours to validate

- **Confidence:** understand which choices are genuinely feasible and what trade-offs each involves.
- **Practical fit:** get a car that works for their actual travel and driver needs. The specific needs must be elicited, not inferred from their work role.
- **Cost control:** understand the points commitment and any additional contribution before committing.
- **Convenience:** complete the choice without repeatedly interrupting work or relying on being online at a particular moment.
- **Fair treatment:** understand why a preferred car may be unavailable and whether an alternative is acceptable.

These are role-level hypotheses derived from the scheme research. Technical employment does not prove confidence with this portal or knowledge of vehicle specifications.

## Frictions and constraints

The available pool may exclude the preferred model, derivative or specification. Eligibility, affordability and collection timing can each rule out an otherwise attractive car. The ordering rush described in older research can favour people who can log in promptly; exact release times and hold periods are not established as current policy. [E6](sources.md#e6-scheme-operations-research) [E7](sources.md#e7-portal-walkthrough)

A recommendation that looks appealing but cannot be ordered would undermine confidence. That consequence is a hypothesis to test, rather than a claim from an employee interview.

## What success could mean

Candidate experience measures, not employment KPIs or agreed targets:

| Outcome | Possible measure |
|---|---|
| A car that meets the employee's needs | Satisfaction after choice and after use; whether essential needs were met. |
| A manageable financial commitment | Understanding of points and contributions; whether the employee's own limit was respected. |
| A convenient decision | Time and repeat visits needed; help required to finish. |
| A dependable renewal | Collection within the required window, with no avoidable gap in transport. |
| A choice they trust | Understanding of compromises and perceived fairness. |

Satisfaction and fairness are themes in the historical research. The measurement methods, baselines and targets remain to be agreed. [E6](sources.md#e6-scheme-operations-research)

## Discussion scenario

For discussion only: the employee is approaching renewal. Their preferred vehicle is unavailable, and several eligible alternatives remain. Explore what makes an alternative acceptable and what they need to understand before choosing. Do not assign a grade, points allowance, family situation or preferred powertrain to make the scenario work.
