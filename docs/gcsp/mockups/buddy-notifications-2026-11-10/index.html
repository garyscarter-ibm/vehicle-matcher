Assumptions and open questions

  • The badge reads offer state, not email delivery, so it matches the offer page and survives a slow email.
  • "Needs you" model: the badge counts open decisions, not unread rows. No read/unread tracking.
  • Badge colour: shown in the Density critical red. The BMW blue accent is the calmer option.
  • Is the short note under the bell (frame 2) worth it for the live moment, or is the badge enough?
  • Should Greg get the same bell later, for releases and new cases?
