# Vehicle offer concept · FUCQ-380

Screens for design discussion. Start at `index.html`; ordinary links open the accept/decline drawers and outcome screens. The small external `gallery.js` is the only interaction: thumbnails replace the featured image in place, and Enlarge opens the selected image with a thumbnail strip, previous/next buttons and arrow-key navigation. Escape or Close returns focus to Enlarge. `accept-validation.html`, `decline-validation.html` and `expired.html` show separate fixed states. No countdown, saving, submission or decision logic. The declaration boxes and reason selections are painted states. The initial acceptance drawer has three unchecked declarations and a disabled final Accept; the accepted screen is separately accessible as `accepted.html`. Documents are short layout copy, not approved scheme wording.

- Timer is **2:00 = two hours**, shown in hours:minutes. Expired is **0:00**. The deadline is illustrative.
- Uses the shared Density heading font, palette and slim portal shell. Drawer sizing/padding follow the current Your cars View record component. The offer uses the full available content width, capped at 1680px.
- Current vehicle is a BMW 320i M Sport Touring; offered vehicle is a BMW 330e M Sport Touring. Car identity, 295/320 points, collection, fitted options and personal details are illustrative visual content. The plug-in hybrid's 410L boot versus 500L makes the drawback clear. These are not verified exact vehicle specifications.
- Benefits and tradeoffs are grouped separately on the same equal three-column desktop grid as the comparison. Benefits occupy the first two columns; extra benefits wrap into further rows. `benefits-expanded.html` directly shows five existing positives individually, without adding navigation to the product. The default keeps two headline benefits. Each expanded benefit has its own tinted cell; an unused grid slot stays page-neutral. Comparison emphasis follows the winning cell: fewer points and the illustrated equipment favour the offer; boot space favours the current car. Ties, powertrain and unknown range stay neutral.
- Paint and upholstery swatches accompany their text labels. Scheme is omitted from the vehicle facts.
- Review states remain direct files, with no design-state navigation inside the product. `gallery-front.html`, `gallery-rear.html` and `gallery-interior.html` are compatible entry screens that open the corresponding larger image over the same offer.
- Options shown side by side are candidates for discussion: heated seats, parking assistance, head-up display, panoramic roof, audio and towbar. The user has not selected which options or differences matter most. High-value option priorities remain for later selection; these visual examples do not set a ranking policy.
- Decline uses the current four fixed categories, Body style, Size, Fuel type and Equipment, including the separate reason-required validation state. Actual labels and codes come from PolicyService (`offer.declineReasons.v1`); these painted states follow `server/gcsp/policy/reviewed/offer-decline-reasons-v1.json`, with Equipment selected in `decline.html`. The concise contact, return and declaration content lives in the acceptance fly-out. Accept and decline are directly visible before the gallery on narrow screens.
