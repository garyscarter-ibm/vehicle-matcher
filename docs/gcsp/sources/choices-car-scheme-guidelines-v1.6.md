## Choices Car Scheme - Introduction

These Guidelines apply to employees of the following BMW Companies herein referred to as the 'Company':

- BMW (UK) Ltd
- BMW Financial Services (GB) Ltd
- BMW UK Pensions Services Ltd
- Park Lane Ltd
- BMW (UK) Holdings Ltd

This document outlines the Guidelines of the Choices Car Scheme, which must be adhered to by all Company employees and eligible drivers electing to participate in the scheme. As well as specifying the Guidelines, this document also provides an explanation of how the scheme works and what is involved in owning and running a Choices vehicle. Participation in the Choices Car Scheme confirms that all users (including eligible drivers) have read these Guidelines and agree to adhere to the terms and conditions of the Scheme. This document is available on the HR Portal - Payments & Benefits - My Benefits - Choices Car Scheme for all the above BMW Group Companies.

The Company acknowledges that buying and running a car is expensive and therefore wishes to offer a complete scheme that will help employees to minimise their expenses. The Choices Car Scheme offers a tax effective way of having a new BMW or MINI.

The Choices Car Scheme covers the cost of buying and running a car, other than fuel costs, and provides or pays for the following:

- Interest Free Loan
- Comprehensive Car Insurance
- Maintenance
- Depreciation Provision
- Vehicle Excise Duty and first registration fee
- Pre-Delivery Inspection Fee
- Delivery and Emergency Service

The above elements are all a benefit in kind and will be subject to tax.

The Company expects all employees and nominated drivers to drive in a sensible, safe, and careful manner at all times. This includes abiding by the speed limits on all employing sites.  The Company reserves the right to take disciplinary action against any car scheme employee who has an accident caused by negligence or careless driving, who has more accidents than should reasonably be expected, or who is convicted of a serious driving offence.  Such disciplinary action may involve the loss of the right to drive Group Car Scheme vehicles and may involve dismissal.  This applies to offences committed inside and outside of normal working hours.

When you take delivery of your vehicle under the Choices Car Scheme you will sign a Loan Agreement ('the Agreement') with Alphabet (GB) Ltd.  Employees must not secure credit or finance against any Choices vehicle.  As long as you comply with the terms and conditions of the Agreement, Alphabet (GB) Ltd will buy-back the vehicle from you at the end of the loan period for the 'Loan Amount' stated in the Agreement.

All vehicles are issued on the understanding that they will be cared for and utilised in a responsible manner and that all the terms of these Guidelines are complied with.  Scheme participants are responsible for the day to day running and maintenance of their vehicles.  This includes all aspects of the vehicle ownership from safety to cleanliness.

You are only entitled to have one vehicle where you are the primary driver. Vehicles ordered for a family member must be ordered in their name in GCSO, if you are entitled to two vehicles and both vehicles will only be for yourself you will need to contact Group Car Schemes so they may validate your request.

The vehicle will be registered to the employee irrespective of who the vehicle is assigned to in the Group Car Scheme Ordering System (GCSO) and therefore the 'Agreement' will always be in the employee's name and the employee must be present for all collection and returns.

## 1. Choices Car Scheme

## 1.1 How does the Choices Car Scheme work?

Employees are provided with an interest free loan to enable them to purchase the Choices vehicle(s).  The Loan Amount provided under the Agreement will be the discounted price at which the vehicle is sold to the employee, which is referred to in the paragraph above.  The interest free loan is treated as a benefit in kind for tax purposes. Income tax is payable by the employee on the amount of interest that would be paid on the loan using the 'Official Rate' of interest as determined by HMRC. Details of the Official Rate are available on the HMRC website.

Employees make a monthly contribution of points or pounds sterling, towards the cost of providing these benefits, based upon the vehicle points in the Choices Points and Eligibility Matrix available on the HR Portal - Payments & Benefits - My Benefits - Choices Car Scheme. Employees should note that this Matrix is updated regularly, so no order should be placed without first referring to this document to check the points cost and availability.

Employees gain from buying their vehicles through the Choices Car Scheme. The exact saving depends upon the model chosen. However, the tax due on the Scheme's benefits is lower than the tax due if an employee were to be provided with a standard Government approved Company vehicle.

You are responsible for taking care of the vehicle, and for payment of all fuel, oil, windscreen washer bottle additive and, if applicable, AdBlue® solution purchases.

Your employing company and Alphabet have the right to inspect your vehicle at any time. Any repairs or modifications made without the authorisation of Alphabet Group Car Schemes will be regarded as unauthorised repairs and may lead to rectification cost and disciplinary action being taken.

## 1.2 Monthly Contributions

Employees wishing to purchase cars with a higher point's value than their entitlement may do so by a cash contribution to cover the outstanding points. Cash contributions are calculated as £1 equal to 1 point and will be deducted from the employee's salary each month. For employees on maternity leave or long-term absence or other types of leave, a cheque or electronic payment may be required for the relevant amount.  Employees should note that their liability for income tax will depend upon whether their benefits are purchased by points or pounds.

Employees should note that HR Services processes any change to Choices point's usage/cash contributions, triggered by the return of one Choices car and the collection of another, at the end of the Agreement. Consequently, any changes to Choices points usage / cash contributions will be recalculated in the month following the car change.

If an employee's Choices points entitlement increases or decreases following a change in role, where applicable their cash contributions will be adjusted accordingly.

In the event that any contributions or other monies due under the Scheme cannot be recovered by way of salary deduction, you will be required to make payment of any such amounts due by cheque or electronic payment immediately upon notice, unpaid amounts being considered a debt.  For payment plans please contact HR Services for details.

## 2. Eligibility

## 2.1 Who is eligible to participate in the scheme?

All Company employees* are eligible to join the Choices Car Scheme providing they are 18 years old or over and subject to meeting the age and licence criteria.  They are allocated a number of monthly benefit points, determined by their personal grade. The points may be used to 'purchase' a range of benefits, including Choices vehicles, which are 'charged' to the employee according to the point's value of the benefit.  Under the Choices Car Scheme, all eligible Company employees are entitled to purchase one car. Restrictions apply based on personal grade, insurance group of the vehicle and the period for which a full driving licence has been held - see Section 2.2 Figure 2.

If you are a G9 or above, you are eligible for a second car for your partner/family member as defined in section 2.  G1 to G8 and Grad 2 are eligible for 1 Choices car.

* Interns, Apprentices and Year 1 Graduates are excluded from the Choices Car Scheme.

The Choices Car Scheme is designed for vehicle use in mainland UK.  However, if you are based overseas, you may be eligible to order a vehicle for use by eligible users in mainland UK providing the number of vehicles worldwide does not exceed your entitlement. You should contact your HRM Department for further details.  If an eligible family member drives the vehicle for more than 50% of the time the vehicle should be ordered in their name on the Group Car Scheme Ordering (GCSO) system.

## 2.2.a     Employee Household Insurance Eligibility (Occasional use)

The following section explains the expansion of the Insurance eligibility for the Group Car Scheme, where it allows for young and/or inexperienced drivers under the age of 25, as part of the Employees immediate household and family tree to drive cars for occasional use. The full list of models included and excluded in this exception, can be found in Appendix C 'Household exception vehicle eligibility list' at the end of this document. This list shall be under the review of the GCS Steering Committee alongside the Insurance Department.

This temporary eligibility applies only to the use of the employee's own scheme car(s) within the employee's immediate household.  The definition of immediate household is that the driver lives and has a driving licence that is registered to the same address as the employee .

This does not allow for drivers in the same household , to order these vehicles through the Group Car Schemes and contracts on these vehicles should not be taken with the intention to be used for the majority of the contract by a Household Occasional driver, this is an exception process for Occasional use only.

There are certain models which are excluded from this and are identified in the list mentioned above, please note this list can change from time to time and therefore is not an exhausted list, if you are unsure, please contact Group Insurance (Motorinsurance@bmw.co.uk) to verify.

## 2.3 Are contract staff included in the Scheme?

No.  Only employees of the Company (with the exception of Interns, Apprentices and Year 1 Graduates), aged 18 or older are eligible to participate in the Choices Car Scheme.

## 3. Ordering and collecting your vehicle

## 3.1 Are there any restrictions on what vehicles I can order?

Scheme participants can select from a comprehensive range of BMW and MINI vehicles with certain usage restrictions may be applied to the following:

- Restriction to model/engine size for less experienced drivers (see Section 2.2) and drivers who have been highlighted as high-risk following information obtained from their licence or claim history.
- Restriction on models available by personal grade.

Your GCSO account will only show vehicles which are available to you.

Availability of certain models, colours and optional extras may be restricted from time to time.  This monitoring of the fleet is essential to the continued viability of the Car scheme.  Details of prices and model availability are available on the HR Portal - Payments & Benefits - My Benefits - Choices Car Scheme.

The Company may require employees in particular roles to drive a car of a certain model appropriate to their position in the Company. Employees will be advised if they are affected by this requirement.

## 3.2 Can I specify optional extras when I order my vehicle, or have options fitted at a later date?

All model derivatives have some factory fitted options included, the quoted prices currently cover for all these costs. No additional options other than Tow Bars and lumbar support as detailed in section 3.5 can be fitted by adding a request in 'Special requirements' when ordering a vehicle.  If the vehicle has gone beyond the stage of amendments in production, you will be advised this request is not possible and will be given the opportunity to cancel the order.  Please contact Alphabet Group Car Schemes if you have any other requirements.

## 8.1 What is the difference between wear & tear and damage requiring an Insurance Claim?

Incidents that are classified as wear and tear do not require an insurance claim to be submitted.

Wear and tear can include:

- Hitting the kerb whilst parking (scuffing the alloy, tyre or underside of the front or side sill).
- Minor Accidental damage caused by items other than vehicles in car parks, i.e., shopping trolleys, pushchairs, or other items (minor dents/scuffs).
- Minor Accidental damage caused by opening doors in car parks where the perpetrator is unknown.
- Interior damage caused by attachments to or clothing i.e., staining, buckles.
- Stone chips.
- Paint damage caused by bird lime, tree sap etc.

Incidents that require an Insurance Claim include:

- Any incident involving a third party.
- Any Road Traffic accident.
- All damage caused by collisions with another party or their property, whether the person/owner is known or not.
- Accidental spillages that are likely to result in significant damage to the vehicle.
- All cases of vandalism or acts of violence.
- Theft of the vehicle and/or its keys.
- Damage caused by outside influences i.e., potholes, car wash machinery/operatives;
- Any incident resulting in damage that detracts from the vehicle's brand representation.

The above is designed to give rough guidance only and is not an exhaustive list. ## 10.4 Grading and Scoring Process (points allocation)

Any damage that is identified during the comprehensive inspection, which has been caused during transportation of the vehicle will be ignored during the process, as will any outstanding, but recorded, insurance claims.

All areas of the vehicle are inspected. Scores are allocated to damage on the vehicle. This differs by damage type, varying from 0 to 5 points .

## 10.4.1  Zero (0) points:

- Minor panel and paint damage which successfully SMART repairs e.g., small dents, small chips to door edge or light scratches.
