# Choices vehicle eligibility matrices (orders from 1 October 2024)

Transcribed by hand from two scanned PDFs in `Box-Box/BMW Employee Car Scheme/`. Both cover the
BMW model range only (no MINI) and show which personal grades may order each engine. Neither
shows points values. ✓ = eligible, ✗ = not eligible. T = "vehicle can be fitted with towbar -
When ordering please detail 'Tow Bar Requested' in GCSO special requirements box."
