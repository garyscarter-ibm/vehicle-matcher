# Order planning — FUCQ-117

This is an additive contract inside the agreed Node prediction component (HLD, **Greg's next best order**). It introduces no process, store, runtime dependency, stock write or order submission. `planOrder(input)` is synchronous and pure. Its service wrapper uses the server-owned context, DemoClock and typed PolicyService. The panel remains FUCQ-119; no screen changes or screenshots are needed here. Feasible, infeasible and alternative presentation fixtures belong to `server/test/fixtures/gcsp-order-plan-outcomes.json`.

## Input and trust boundary

The resolved input has `runId`, ISO `asOf`, `versions` with nonempty `forecast`, `policy`, `stock`, `supply`, `cohort` and `catalog` pins, and:

- `forecast`: `basis`, ISO `horizonEnd`, `limitations`, and `entitlements`. Each row has a unique `entitlementId`, `employeeId`, `renewalId`, `entitlementSlotId`, exact `renewalAt`, joint `acceptableDerivativeIds`, and `gates`. Optional `requiredBy` is a confirmed deadline no later than that renewal; matching uses it without replacing the renewal fact. Optional unique `excludedStockIds` excludes only those exact units for that employee. One employee may occur once in this horizon. Acceptable choices already passed shared eligibility, essentials, terms and decline gates. Empty choices retain unmet needs; they never become permission to relax a gate.
- `catalog`: exact `derivativeId`, `modelId`, nonnegative integer `unitCostMinor` and nonnegative integer `leadTimeDays`. Costs use the declared policy currency, and lead times are resolved policy inputs, never guesses about confirmed arrivals.
- `stock`: unique `stockId`, exact `derivativeId`, ISO `availableAt` no later than `asOf`, and nullable `heldForEntitlementId`. Optional `permittedEntitlementIds` restrict exact-unit visibility; omitted/null means the trusted resolved input grants the whole cohort visibility. A held unit can only satisfy that entitlement. Allocated stock is excluded by the producer. Stock ownership and actual release provenance are producer obligations.
- `inbound`: unique `supplyId`, `derivativeId`, positive integer `quantity`, integer `releasedQuantity` between zero and quantity, exact ISO `expectedAt`, and `state: confirmed`. Only the unreleased remainder counts; requests and delays do not count. Provenance is retained in the input digest.
- `constraints`: integer `horizonDays`, `budgetMinor`, declared `currency`, complete `modelBounds` and `derivativeBounds` rows (`min`, `max`), and nonnegative `objectiveWeights` (`coverage`, `unallocatedStock`, `costMinor`). At least one weight is positive. Bounds apply to NEW orders, not existing stock. The horizon must equal `asOf + horizonDays`; no renewal beyond it enters this input. A current draft, if present, is `{ quantities: [{ derivativeId, quantity }] }`.

The kernel refuses malformed, incomplete, duplicate or unknown identities. Inputs contain exact trusted quantities; privacy-banded public aggregates are not accepted. It does not read a file, clock, repository or policy itself.

## Algorithm and result

The solver exhaustively searches bounded integer order vectors, enforcing derivative/model bounds, budget and arrival within the horizon. For each feasible vector, augmenting-path capacity matching maximises acceptable entitlement coverage by each effective deadline (`requiredBy ?? renewalAt`). Matching can move a flexible employee to their substitute, preserving scarce stock for an employee with one acceptable choice. It cannot count an employee twice, allocate another employee's hold or reuse their unchanged declined exact unit. Another unit of the same derivative remains eligible.

The objective is `coverageWeight × covered − unallocatedStockWeight × unused − costMinorWeight × spendMinor`. Coverage here is deterministic acceptable feasibility, not a learned acceptance probability or a promise of eventual selection. `expectedAcceptableCoverage` states this basis. Risk is supply inside the horizon left unmatched to the supplied cohort; it is not a calibrated probability of unwanted stock. Forecast limitations carry through. Ties prefer lower spend, fewer orders, then the lexicographic derivative quantity vector.

Unmet explanations check whether one additional acceptable vehicle could arrive and satisfy every hard bound. If it could, `objective-tradeoff` explains the deliberate uncovered need. Otherwise the relevant lead-time, derivative/model maximum or budget constraint is named. A budget can bind an integer purchase even when the remaining currency amount is nonzero but insufficient for another acceptable vehicle.

Results have `contractVersion`, stable input-derived `planId`, `versions`, `status` and a solver record. Feasible results include integer `quantities`, `modelQuantities`, `allocations`, coverage, risk, unmet needs with gates/constraint references, binding constraints and objective components. `alternatives` always evaluates the zero-new-order baseline (and a distinct feasible runner-up when one exists). A baseline/draft can be infeasible; its conflicts remain visible. A single feasible vector is explicitly identified, not presented as two distinct choices. `currentDraftComparison` uses the same exact inputs and hard constraints when supplied.

Infeasible results contain no recommended quantities; `conflicts` names the contradictory constraints. The conflict set is explanatory and non-minimal, not an asserted minimal unsatisfiable core. Missing/invalid inputs return `invalid`. Safety limits return `unsupported`, with no partial vector described as feasible or optimal. The exact documented limits are exported as `SOLVER_LIMITS`; effort exhaustion is deterministic, not wall-clock dependent.

## Read-only integration ports

`createOrderPlanService({ policy, identity, clock, forecast, supply, catalog }).readPlan(context, { currentDraft? })` requires Greg through F84, resolves the canonical policy, reads the three trusted producers twice and fences clock/run/content changes. It never accepts browser identity/run selectors or writes repository state. There is no built-in fake forecast or catalog. The trusted forecast port is `readForecast(context, read)` returning `{runId, asOf, policyVersion, version, cohortVersion, forecast}`. Its entitlement rows additionally supply `companyId`/`schemeId` for exact stock visibility. The catalog port is `readCatalog(context, read)` returning `{runId, asOf, policyVersion, version, currency, derivatives}`; its cost currency must match the canonical budget currency.

`createOrderPlanSupplyProvider({ repository, identity, clock, catalog }).readSupply(context, read)` reads the actual five F104/repository collections (`stock`, `holds`, `future_order_requests`, `confirmed_supply`, `event_receipts`) through query ports, twice. `resolveSupplyRecords` is its pure converter. Catalog derivatives must explicitly bind `{production: {model, derivative}, configurationIds}` to planner IDs. Ambiguous/missing bindings fail closed. The converter retains source visibility and `heldForEntitlementSlotId`; the service resolves these against the trusted cohort to kernel entitlement IDs and exact permitted-visibility lists. A hold outside the cohort remains unavailable to everyone else. Unresolved/expired physical holds do not silently free stock.

The provider's `versions.stock` digests stock and holds; `versions.supply` digests request/confirmation/release evidence. These are intentionally stronger planner snapshot identities than a raw stock-only digest. Requests, acknowledgements, successful release receipts and allocated stock remain distinct. A confirmed quantity already released/allocated is subtracted from inbound even though its exact unit is excluded from free stock. No request-wide release total is guessed across acknowledgements.

## Trusted saved-demand extension and current source gate

`createExpressedOrderForecastProvider({repository, identity, clock, catalog, futureChoices})` is the service's default forecast provider when no alternative forecast is injected. It reads the actual authoritative F99 saved briefs internally, keeps each employee's joint choice set, and leaves the public privacy-banded projection unchanged. An unspecified saved `renewalId` (`null` in BriefService's published contract) is resolved through the exact employee and entitlement-slot pair; an explicit conflicting renewal or slot fails closed. Known renewal dates come only from the current contract's `contractEndDate`, interpreted at UTC end of day as in F112. Unknown dates fail closed. Already allocated exact selections and renewals beyond the horizon are excluded with reasons.

The future port is `futureChoices.evaluateChoices(context,{read, employee, brief, catalog, history})`. Its successful value includes `{runId, policyVersion, employeeId, briefVersion, catalogVersion, version, acceptableDerivativeIds, excludedStockIds, requiredBy, gates, decisions, limitations}`. The forecast reads and fences actual repository `decisions`, `offers` and `briefs` as well as employee/slot/stock/hold inputs; history is not accepted from the public demand DTO.

`createFutureChoiceEvaluator({policy, identity, terms})` is a dedicated plan-owned implementation. It uses the real PolicyService eligibility, allowance and exact configuration points reads, plus the shared pure brief-validation, essentials, charging, confirmed-fact and preference functions. It never constructs a VehicleSnapshot, exact-stock identity or current availability for future configurations. Trusted Greg identity is checked before and after evaluation. Missing ports or incompatible facts, terms, policy/version/employee/renewal pins fail closed.

The injected catalog must explicitly contain `configurations:[{configurationId, derivativeId, version, display:{name}, facts}]`. Its derivative rows list the exact corresponding `configurationIds`; both directions, uniqueness and membership are checked, with at most256 represented configurations. Configuration rows reject root `orderUuid`, `stockId`, `vin`, `identityKind` and `availability` fields. Facts carry supported known/unknown values, units and versioned provenance observed no later than `asOf`. The supply adapter separately requires each derivative's explicit `production:{model, derivative}` mapping. Names never create identities.

The trusted future-terms port is `terms.readFutureTerms(context,{read, employee, brief, catalogVersion, configuration, renewalAt})`. Success returns `{status:"ok", value:{runId, asOf, policyVersion, employeeId, renewalId, briefVersion, catalogVersion, configurationId, version, terms}}`, where `terms` passes the shared EmployeeTerms constructor. The exact pins must match; `validFrom <= asOf`, `validUntil > renewalAt`, points and allowance equal canonical policy reads, and assurance is permitted by the canonical recommendation policy. Missing prices cannot become free cars; no fallback terms/costs or pending proposal values are used.

A derivative-only order or inbound quantity cannot identify which represented configuration will arrive. Consequently **every represented configuration must pass hard gates** before that derivative enters an employee's acceptable set. Each configuration retains its individual decision and evidence. Preference fit remains advisory: low scores and unconfirmed preferences do not invent an acceptance threshold or probability.

A recorded decline joins its actual offer to the exact `orderUuid` and the historical confirmed brief version. Unchanged business intent keeps that exact unit excluded. Timestamps, provenance and equipment-list order cannot reopen it, and inferred/unconfirmed changes retain the previous confirmed intent for this comparison. A relevant confirmed business change allows reevaluation; expiry is not dislike. A missing historical confirmed brief conservatively preserves exclusion; an unresolved offer identity makes evaluation unavailable. Future configurations and other exact cars of the same derivative are not excluded by that decline.

The actual current contract supplies `renewalAt` using the existing strict UTC end-of-day interpretation. A known employee or confirmed saved-brief `requiredBy` can only narrow it; a later date never extends renewal. A provided unknown/unconfirmed employee deadline prevents coverage rather than inventing one. If no earlier deadline is supplied, actual renewal is the deadline. Matching, new-order lead-time checks, allocations and unmet explanations use this effective deadline and distinguish an arrival after required-by from an arrival after renewal. Future terms remain conservatively valid through actual renewal.
