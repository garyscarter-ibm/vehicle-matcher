# FUCQ-61 downstream impact — 7 November 2026

FUCQ-61 supplies an existing corpus that downstream stories can reuse. FUCQ-77 remains necessary for scheme coverage and configurator proof; it no longer needs to collect a separate demo corpus from scratch. No other story's distinct product outcome is made redundant by the configuration count.

Russell requested the FUCQ-77 approach change, removal of its Blocks relationship to FUCQ-78, and an audit of other affected stories. This record implements those two story changes and records the wider audit for the existing claim owners.

**Changes applied.** FUCQ-77 is now **Verify scheme coverage using the collected BMW configuration corpus**. Start with FUCQ-61's retained records and original evidence, identify missing coverage or evidence, and collect or enrich only those gaps. Jira link `62530` (FUCQ-77 **blocks** FUCQ-78) was deleted. FUCQ-78's description metadata and local `depends_on` now agree with that removal; its integration inputs and acceptance criteria remain. Both issues remain In Progress with their existing assignee and claims. Fresh Jira reads verified both descriptions and the exact remaining link sets; the [readback](evidence/f61-downstream-impact-2026-11-07/jira-readback.json) retains the checks.

FUCQ-77 still requires all seven acceptance areas: every matrix model line mapped or explicitly excluded; at least eight distinct configurations per mapped line and 200 overall; the existing identity and constructibility checks; truthful technical facts and images; at least 20 actual headless configurator round trips; the original safety limits; and versioned publication through the existing loader. Reused records must satisfy the same checks as newly collected records. Any new gap collection keeps the original 3,000-request/concurrency-two limit. Historical FUCQ-61 source traffic remains separately evidenced and is not represented as fitting within FUCQ-77's budget. The unconditional fresh collection step is now conditional on the coverage audit finding missing configurations.

**What the delivered corpus establishes.** The [FUCQ-61 closeout](evidence/fucq61/exact10000-closeout-2026-11-07/README.md) records exactly 10,000 selected complete resolved source configurations from 10,351 admitted distinct configurations, with canonical identities and retained source provenance. The cohort has 43 VG and 26 AG codes. A complete resolved factory selection does not establish a semantic model/engine mapping, scheme eligibility, points price, stock availability or employee choice outcome. Semantic model name, body and powertrain remain unknown across the source inventory; the five recorded technical facts are unknown across those rows, and only two representative HEROFRONT image associations are registered. FUCQ-77 must assess actual coverage and enrichment gaps rather than infer them from the count.

The [current F58–63 status](f58-63-morning-status-2026-11-07.md) and [actual FUCQ-62 live readback](evidence/fucq62/fleet-dot12-linux-live-run42372746118/README.md) record the later successful `.12` deployment and lookup observation.
