# Presenter release statistics, 11 November 2026

Gary requested five counters: **Latest release**, **Total released**, **Unallocated**, **Buddy Allocated** and **Manually Allocated**. He also requested chart skeletons during a release and the chart disclosure heading **Latest release stats**. The authorised refinement is recorded in `docs/gcsp/presenter-revamp-spec.md`.

The latest counter describes the newest batch. Total released counts distinct cars in completed release receipts for the current run. The other three counters partition those cars by current availability and retained reservation/selection route. Current reservations and pending reconciliation count under Buddy or Manual; confirmed selections retain that route. Historical released, expired or failed holds do not count. Missing stock or an unexplained occupied car makes the read unavailable rather than inventing an allocation route. No employee identifiers are returned.

The presenter uses existing `factList`, `countBars` and `loadingSkeleton` components. Skeletons start before the release request and remain through the refreshed stats read, including failure recovery. An open disclosure remains open, with its summary focus preserved. The five counters use one row at desktop width and the existing two-column arrangement at narrow width.
