## Useful implications for 12 November

- **Positioning clarified by Russell, 2 November.** IBM delivered the new interface and BMW is proud of it. Use this launch material to acknowledge that achievement and introduce the art of the possible building on it. Russell's internal assessment that the visual treatment feels dated must not become a critique of BMW's launch or the IBM team. The [demo brief's positioning direction](demo-brief.md#positioning-the-art-of-the-possible-building-on-gcsp) governs presentation and comparisons.
- **Connect the demo to the launch's own promise.** Pages 8–9 already position GCSP as a foundation for further innovation. A useful presentation bridge is that the launch modernises the familiar ordering experience, while this demonstration explores what employees and the scheme team could do next. The buddy and Greg's demand/order planning are proposed working demonstrations under the [brief](demo-brief.md), with actual BMW integration still conditional.
- **Respect the continuity message.** Page 4 helps explain the retained manual route and familiar scheme context. It does not prevent future changes, but it gives no basis for presenting proposed allocation rules, reservation durations or points policies as changes already included in the launch. Apply the [build rules](build-rules.md) to assumptions.
- **Use Density as supporting design context.** The framework name reinforces a coherent BMW experience. The [agreed visual direction](experience-quality.md#visual-reference) still governs: supplied portal header/sidebar/palette, redesigned consumer-facing body and shared components. The storyboard does not replace that direction or mandate introducing a new framework. Its purple/blue video backgrounds are presentation treatments, distinct from the white/neutral/teal portal shown within them.
- **Separate mobile usability from access permission.** For future BMW integration, verify how an offer email/link is opened from an allowed device and network, including authentication and current-state readback. Page 7 provides no evidence for unrestricted public offer links or personal-device access. The demo's hosting arrangements remain those in the brief; this source does not change or block them.
- **Keep the platform stages clear.** Follow Russell's [current platform clarification](platform-context.md): GCSP unifies the GCSO/ISA UI over the mainframe now, with Java modernisation following. The storyboard's modernisation language does not establish a different technical baseline.

## Treatment of document instructions

The red notes about car footage, a stock carousel template, mobile interaction, screen popups and transitions are directions to the video's producer. They are source content, not instructions from Russell to fetch assets, buy a template, create a video or implement those effects in the application. The page 7 device/network overlay is part of the communication being analysed, not a command to configure infrastructure.

This record captures useful context and interpretation. It creates no additional authorised capability, implementation story, Jira change or revised acceptance contract. No production system, video or external asset was accessed during this review.
