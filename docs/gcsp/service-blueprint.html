Paula’s next company car

She likes her BMW 320i M Sport Touring. She wants the next car to fit her life, without choosing it becoming a second job.

Follow her journey

Meet Paula Robinson, BMW Financial Services. Capable, organised and quietly particular. She likes a good car, a well-planned week and getting outside at the weekend. Her Portimao Blue Metallic 320i M Sport Touring takes walking kit, overnight bags and the occasional awkward purchase without a puzzle.

Home is Winchcombe; work takes her to Summit ONE in Farnborough. Those Cotswolds-to-Hampshire trips make comfort and dependable longer journeys matter. Around home, the same car can feel large to park.

Her working week*
Two planned office days; occasional workshops.
Her current car
BMW 320i M Sport Touring (G21 LCI2). B48 2.0-litre petrol · automatic · Portimao Blue Metallic.
Her electric-car question*
A driveway, no chargepoint yet. What would a workable plan involve?

*Authored scenario. BMW advertises hybrid working in some Farnborough roles; Paula’s attendance arrangement and charging feasibility are unverified.

Paula’s experienceHer actions and what she sees · her agent takes over at 04a

Paula does

  1. Opens the renewal email, clicks “My car and what’s next” and signs in.
  2. Chooses “Use my connected-car summary” if permission is needed, then opens her personal car view.
  3. Checks the usage period and return deadline; clicks “Explore available cars”.

Paula sees

Her Portimao Blue Metallic BMW 320i M Sport Touring fills the screen. Cabin views celebrate the things she enjoys: the heated steering wheel, memory seat, panoramic roof and Harman/Kardon audio listed on her record. “Your last 30 days” adds miles driven, average MPG, an efficiency trend and her longest recorded journey. Remaining agreement miles and service status sit alongside. “What could be next?” invites her to explore replacements from this personal starting point.

A familiar car becomes the starting point for something worth looking forward to.

Paula does

  1. Browses the eligible-car gallery and opens an appealing car’s photos and details.
  2. Sees that she would still need to check releases and act before someone else selects it.
  3. Leaves without reserving or ordering and clicks “Let my agent look for me”.

Paula sees

Large vehicle photographs, paint and cabin views make the manual portal feel much more inviting. She can compare an available car with her 320i M Sport Touring. But attractive tiles do not change the familiar release-day problem: cars can disappear while she is in a meeting. A clear alternative offers to keep looking for her, using the same stock.

She can enjoy browsing without signing up to another Monday scramble.

Paula does

  1. Chooses “Comfort” and “Space” from six broad priorities: Comfort, Space, Easy parking, Running costs, Driving enjoyment and Electric driving.
  2. Selects “Easier parking” as the one thing she would improve, then clicks “Continue”.

Paula sees

Her 320i M Sport Touring stays beside six illustrated choices, rather than a wall of technical features. Two selected tiles become a simple sentence: “Keep the comfortable journeys and useful space; make parking easier.” She can correct it immediately. There are no feature-by-feature ratings and no requirement to invent a new ideal car.

A few recognisable choices give her agent direction without turning Paula into a vehicle specialist.

Paula does

  1. Reviews the prefilled drivers, return window and practical needs. Confirms what is still right and changes only what is wrong.
  2. Sets the most extra she would pay each month, including “£0 extra”, and answers one charging question: “Not yet”.
  3. Checks the one-screen brief and clicks “Save brief and start my agent”, allowing searching and temporary holds.

Paula sees

A brief she can read at a glance, with her 320i M Sport Touring as the reference. Known facts are already there; only gaps need an answer. “Add a must-have” opens extra questions only when needed. Her maximum extra payment and “No plug-in options yet” are easy to spot. The final button changes to “Your agent is on it”, with a direct link to its activity.

She checks a useful starting point instead of completing a personality questionnaire.

Paula does

  1. Opens “Agent activity” once to check her brief is active.
  2. Closes the portal and gets back to work.

Paula sees

A calm activity view: “Your brief is saved. I’m checking available cars.” Her Comfort / Space priorities, parking improvement and extra-payment limit sit beside her 320i M Sport Touring. A last-checked time replaces repeated refreshing. “Edit brief” and “Pause searching” stay close by, so handing over still feels under her control.

She leaves with confidence that work is happening without her watching the screen.

The agent does

  1. Reads her approved brief and the cars actually released to her scheme.
  2. Removes cars that fail her entitlement, driver rules, must-haves, extra-payment limit or collection window.
  3. Compares suitable cars with her 320i M Sport Touring and ranks them by her priorities. Follows the agreed rule for which employee gets a hold.
  4. Requests a hold on the best suitable car. After confirmation, prepares her comparison and sends the offer.

The agent sees

A working record for each car: available or unavailable, fits or fails each essential, monthly contribution, and the comfort / space / parking differences from her 320i M Sport Touring. A held-car reference and expiry show when it can safely tell Paula “This one is reserved for you”. Unknown equipment remains a gap, not a guessed benefit.

The checks that normally consume Paula’s attention happen before she is asked to decide.

Paula does

  1. Opens “Your first car recommendation” in her email and clicks “View my recommendation”.
  2. Opens the paint and cabin gallery, then clicks “Compare with my 320i M Sport Touring”.
  3. Checks the monthly contribution, the compromises and how long her reservation lasts.

Paula sees

A photographed car is already held for her, with its exact model, paint and interior leading the page. “Why this one?” connects it to her priorities. Beside her 320i M Sport Touring, she can explore space and parking differences, then see “kept / changed / not confirmed” for familiar comforts such as the heated wheel, memory seat, roof and audio. The monthly extra payment and “Held until” time stay visible while she browses.

She gets the enjoyable part of car shopping after the tedious checks have already happened.

Paula does

  1. Decides the extra payment is not worth this particular car, even though it is within her original limit.
  2. Clicks “Decline this car”, selects “Extra monthly payment is too high” and confirms “Decline and keep searching”.
  3. Checks the saved card in “My rejections” below the confirmation, then reads the follow-up question.

Paula sees

The car becomes a photo card in “My rejections”, with its model, quote, her reason and the date. She can reopen the comparison with her 320i M Sport Touring without rebuilding it. “Your agent is still looking” gives her a clear way forward. The history remembers her decision; it does not leave a tempting “Choose” button on an offer she has already declined.

Saying no leaves a useful memory, rather than a lost offer or a search to start again.

Paula does

  1. Reads “Would you like to lower your maximum extra monthly payment?” on the same offer page.
  2. Enters a lower £ amount, checks the old and new limits, and clicks “Update brief and keep searching”.

Paula sees

A small before-and-after panel changes just one line of her brief. Her 320i M Sport Touring, priorities and must-haves stay in place. A new activity entry says the remaining cars have been checked against the lower limit; in this story none fits yet. “Watching for the next release” means she can leave, rather than scroll through cars she has just ruled out.

The next search changes for a reason Paula can see and approve.

Paula does

  1. Opens “If I’m still waiting” from Agent activity.
  2. Looks at the help choices, then closes the panel and leaves her agent running.

Paula sees

A compact set of reassuring choices: browse the same eligible stock herself, revisit a flexible preference, talk to a named scheme-team contact, or ask whether a later car / approved extension could work. Each option explains what it would change. She takes none of them here. Her 320i M Sport Touring and its actual deadline remain in view, so the panel feels connected to her situation.

There is a visible way to get help without being pushed into a different journey.

Paula does

  1. Continues with her day while the next stock release is checked.
  2. If she opens Agent activity, checks the new release entry and recommendation status.

Paula sees

Her activity gains a useful line: “New cars checked using your updated limit.” It then changes to “A new recommendation is held for you” only when the car is secured. The page carries forward her 320i M Sport Touring comparison and saved priorities, without sending her back through setup.

The service remembers both the car she likes and the feedback she has just given.

Paula does

  1. Opens the second recommendation email and clicks “View my recommendation”.
  2. Opens “What changed since the first offer”, then compares the new car with her 320i M Sport Touring.
  3. Explores its photos, checks the lower extra payment and the remaining hold time.

Paula sees

“You asked for a lower extra payment” leads the new offer, followed by the exact reduction between the two quotes. The same clear gallery and comparison with her 320i M Sport Touring show what she keeps and what she gives up. Comfort, space and easier parking remain visible priorities; a compromise is named rather than hidden behind a glowing match score.

Her second offer visibly reflects her decision, without making her explain herself again.

Paula does

  1. Clicks “Choose this car” on the second offer.
  2. Checks the exact car, monthly points / extra payment, dates and required terms.
  3. Confirms the acknowledgements and clicks “Submit my selection”.

Paula sees

The car she has chosen takes centre stage with a simple confirmation: “Selection received”. A clear next-step strip replaces the countdown with approval progress, her reference and an “Order progress” link. Her 320i M Sport Touring stays labelled “Current car” until the physical swap; choosing its replacement does not erase the information she still needs.

A considered choice becomes visible progress, with no doubt about what happens next.

Paula does

  1. Clicks “Order progress” from her selection acknowledgement.
  2. Completes only outstanding driver / eligibility checks or other requested actions.
  3. Checks the approval result and the next step to arrange handover.

Paula sees

The replacement’s image follows her into a progress view split into “For you” and “With the scheme team”. Completed tasks drop out of her to-do list; the next action stays prominent. She can see who owns a delay and when the order is approved. Her current 320i M Sport Touring’s return date sits alongside the replacement plan.

She sees the new car getting closer without having to chase every team involved.

Paula does

  1. Opens “Arrange handover”, chooses a workable collection / return slot and clicks “Request this slot”.
  2. Checks the provider’s confirmation, adds the appointment to her calendar and reviews the checklist.
  3. When required documents arrive, opens the signing link and completes them.

Paula sees

A handover plan brings both cars together: return her 320i M Sport Touring and collect the replacement, with each appointment clearly requested or confirmed. A calendar entry, location details and a short preparation checklist make the change feel real. If a date moves, she sees the effect on both arrangements and the person helping to resolve it.

The main journey ends with a car chosen and a practical plan she can put in her diary.

Behind the scenesWho does what, and the result
  • The service

    Brings together her 320i M Sport Touring, agreement and permitted usage summary. Dates come from her agreement; MPG comes from observed trips, not a brochure.

  • Connected-data provider

    Supplies dated summaries only if access is authorised. Missing data is labelled; Paula can continue without connecting her car.

  • Scheme team

    Owns renewal timing and resolves incorrect car or contract details.

ResultA personal current-car view and the correct renewal deadline.

  • The service

    Shows the same eligible stock in a clearer gallery. Opening a photo or comparison does not reserve a car.

  • Stock system

    Remains the source of availability. A refreshed screen can show a car has gone; better presentation alone does not solve competition for stock.

  • Paula’s agent

    Starts only after she sets her brief and gives permission in 03. Browsing creates no hidden preference or order.

ResultPaula chooses delegated searching; nothing has been reserved.

  • The service

    Saves up to two broad priorities and one optional improvement. They guide the order of suitable cars; they do not silently become strict requirements.

  • Vehicle-data owner

    Supplies the checked dimensions and equipment used for comparison with the 320i M Sport Touring. Photos cannot prove fitted features.

  • Paula

    Confirms any strict space or accessibility need in the short practical check next.

ResultTwo priorities and one improvement, ready for a practical check.

  • The service

    Reuses confirmed drivers and dates. Asks only for missing facts that could change eligibility or practical fit; any unknown essential must be resolved before a suitable offer.

  • Scheme rules

    Check entitlement, driver restrictions and the payment rules for her scheme. The extra-payment question is shown only when her scheme permits a contribution.

  • Paula’s agent

    Receives the approved brief and search / hold permission. It cannot order a car or change her limits on its own.

ResultBrief 1 is approved; searching and temporary holds are permitted.

  • The service

    Starts the search from the brief Paula approved. Records meaningful progress and the last successful stock check.

  • Paula’s agent

    Keeps searching while her permission is active. If a feed fails, the activity view says checking is delayed instead of claiming there are no cars.

  • Scheme team

    Receives persistent connection problems and renewal risks; Paula is not asked to diagnose them.

ResultAn active search with visible progress and clear controls.

  • Paula’s agent

    Uses her saved answers to rank suitable cars, explain the trade-offs and ask for a temporary reservation.

  • Stock system

    Confirms whether that exact car is held exclusively for Paula. If someone else got it first, the agent checks the next suitable car.

  • Notification service

    Sends the recommendation only after the hold is confirmed. If nothing fits, the agent keeps watching and shows why.

ResultA confirmed first hold and a prepared recommendation, or an honest “Still looking”.

  • The service

    Creates an offer page from the confirmed car, price and hold. Compares verified details with her 320i M Sport Touring; model images are labelled when they are representative.

  • Notification service

    Sends an email with a secure link and puts the same recommendation in her account. Email carries no sensitive personal usage detail.

  • Stock system

    Supplies the current hold status. An old or delayed link shows what is true now; it cannot extend the reservation.

ResultPaula has a checked offer she can enjoy exploring and decide on.

  • The service

    Records her reason against this offer and adds it to “My rejections”. Repeated clicks do not create more decisions.

  • Stock system

    Releases the exact held car back to the shared eligible pool, so another employee or their agent may select it under the normal allocation rules. If confirmation is delayed, it stays pending and is not reassigned.

  • Paula’s agent

    Keeps running and skips the same unchanged car for her. A decline gives neither a penalty nor extra priority.

ResultA recorded rejection, a released car and one focused follow-up.

  • The service

    Uses her selected reason to show a fixed budget question. It waits for her new figure and confirmation before changing anything.

  • Paula’s agent

    Checks the remaining released cars again using the lower limit and the same must-haves. None fits in this story, so it watches for more stock.

  • Scheme team

    Sees grouped unmet needs to inform future supply. Paula’s personal usage history is not a team performance score.

ResultBrief 2 has the lower limit; the rejected car is excluded and searching continues.

  • The service

    Keeps help available while the search runs. Simply opening this panel does not change the brief, reserve another car or request an extension.

  • Scheme team

    Takes ownership if Paula asks for help or her renewal reaches an agreed risk threshold, then gives her a named contact and next update.

  • Alphabet / authorised owner

    Decides whether an extension or alternative arrangement is allowed. Future requests, confirmed future supply and released cars remain distinct.

ResultHelp stays available; Paula continues with the same active search.

  • Paula’s agent

    Receives the release, checks it against brief 2 and skips the unchanged car she declined. It applies the same eligibility, driver, practical and payment limits.

  • Stock system

    Confirms a hold on the best suitable car under the agreed allocation rules. An unsuccessful hold sends the agent back to searching.

  • The service

    Prepares the second comparison only after confirmation, including what differs from the first recommendation.

ResultA second suitable car is held within her revised limit.

  • The service

    Compares the saved first quote with the second quote and highlights the confirmed payment change. It does not claim to have discovered an unspoken preference.

  • Paula’s agent

    Explains the second car against her approved priorities and her 320i M Sport Touring, using the same checked facts as the offer page.

  • Notification service

    Sends the new secure link and account alert. The page always checks the current hold and expiry.

ResultPaula can judge the revised offer and make the final choice.

  • The service

    Checks that her hold and quoted terms are still valid, records her choice once and asks the scheme’s ordering process to take it forward.

  • Ordering system

    Returns a reference or a clear pending result. If a reply is missing, the service checks what happened before trying again.

  • Paula’s agent

    Stops making new holds for this renewal. Paula’s selection still needs the required approvals; the agent has not placed an independent order.

ResultHer selection is acknowledged and passed into the approval process.

  • Ordering service

    Sends the selection to the required scheme / HR / manager checks and tracks their replies. Only outstanding employee tasks appear for Paula.

  • Scheme team

    Resolves blocked approvals and provides a next update. The dated guide limits changes after order approval.

  • Document provider

    Tracks the required agreements and signatures. Booking may happen before documents arrive; missing signatures still block handover.

ResultAn approved order, a short task list and visible document status.

  • Appointment provider

    Checks capacity and confirms the slot. A request is not shown as a firm booking before that reply.

  • The service

    Combines booking, document and vehicle-readiness updates into one plan. It keeps her 320i M Sport Touring as the current car until return and replacement collection are confirmed.

  • Scheme team

    Resolves a delayed car or changed date. Physical handover requires completed documents and a ready vehicle.

ResultA confirmed handover plan; collection and later use are outside this walkthrough.
