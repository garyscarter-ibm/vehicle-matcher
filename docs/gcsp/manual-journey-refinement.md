## Experience and information architecture

Keep the screenshot-based slim branded header, account context, narrow sidebar and white/grey/neutral/teal palette. Use labelled, functioning navigation for implemented destinations; the screenshots do not establish every icon's destination. Breadcrumbs are optional.

The body follows the consumer Used Cars experience: substantial vehicle images, precise hierarchy, generous useful spacing, cards and readable facts, clear actions and understandable consequences. These five content groups are an intended hierarchy, not five mandatory pages:

1. **Your cars and contracts.** A strong current-car image and model/specification, actual contract end/countdown, mileage/term, scheme/driver and cost context; a clear New order or Replace action when permitted. Retain account/licence/additional-driver details and scheme histories in accessible detail sections.
2. **Your order choices.** Confirm known scheme/employee facts, choose permitted driver, contract+mileage and requested collection location/date. Explain allowance and any authorised cash contribution. Avoid repetitive entry of known facts.
3. **Available cars.** Visual series/body navigation and exact-car cards, text search and sorting; show the full permitted released-stock pool. Make colour/interior, points/contribution, availability and useful specification visible. Expand technical data/options without hiding decision-critical facts.
4. **Review this car.** An explicit action acquires the exact car through the stock authority. Show real hold state, remaining time and consequences, required contact and replacement return-mileage information, plus optional bounded special requirements.
5. **Check and submit.** Readable review sections, edit paths, complete versioned declarations and intentional submission. Show the generated reference and actual approval/handoff state, Print and Finish. A pending approval is not a completed approval or confirmed appointment.

There are useful alternatives, but less suitable ones: copying the old seven-page layout would retain unnecessary density; replacing it with the buddy flow would lose the manual baseline. The chosen direction preserves the employee's manual decisions while redesigning composition and controls.

## Implemented manual context contract · FUCQ-65

`server/gcsp/manual-context/` implements backend account, draft and stock browsing. `ManualContextService` receives the existing repository, `PolicyService`, `DemoClock`, `DemoIdentityAdapter` and five replaceable read ports: `employeeContract`, `driver`, `schemeTerms`, `collectionRule` and `releasedStock`. Their `read(context, { runId, asOf, policyVersion })` results use the shared `status` discriminant. This adds no identity resolver, datastore, dependency or screen.

Account and driver envelopes contain trusted `runId`, `environmentId`, `employeeId`, coherent `recordSetId` and source `{ mode, sourceRef, sourceVersion, schemaVersion, effectiveFrom, observedAt }`. Company, grade and scheme must match identity authority. Contract/history rows preserve exact employee/scheme/slot IDs. Account/driver local repository revisions agree when both are supplied. Future-effective, incompatible, malformed or unavailable rows fail closed. The repository adapter reads the existing seeded employee's `contract.manualContext` JSON and entitlement slots; this is an additive payload convention, not a schema migration. External adapters map their payloads to the same contract.

`readAccount(context)` distinguishes `absent-hr-account`, `no-entitlement`, `no-contract`, `unknown-scheme`, `unknown-grade` and `unsupported-scheme`; source outage is `status: unavailable`. A usable new-order entitlement has `state: ready`, `contractState: no-contract` and Order. A missing contract referenced by a slot is the blocking `no-contract` state. A ready current contract offers Replace. Each history retains `history`/`empty-history`, count and `orderingSupported`; unsupported supplied history stays readable. Initial ordering is BMW/Choices FS&UK under the explicitly selected October 2024 matrix.

The account projection supplies driver/licence summaries, choices, typed allowance, assumption/source versions, opaque `contextVersion`, actions and `view`. Account/licence fields are read-only and owned by logical `hr-support`/`driver-support` routes. Driver, contract, collection, contact, expected-return-mileage and special-requirements fields declare editability/support ownership. Support IDs are consumer routing contracts, not live support integrations.

`saveDraft(context, { commandId, expectedDraftVersion, expectedContextVersion, choices })` accepts only registered fields. Choices contain new/replacement mode, old contract ID, trusted slot/scheme, supplied driver, contract option (term and annual mileage), requested collection date/location, BMW brand, contact, expected return mileage and bounded special requirements. Replacement requires the exact current contract and return mileage; new orders cannot borrow either. Current contract dates and odometer remain separate from requested collection and expected return mileage. Invalid combinations return field reasons and a validation view.

Assumed key `manual.context.v1` is the single revisable synthetic configuration for supported schemes/brands; six-month/8,000 and twelve-month/12,000 mile-per-year options; permitted drivers/locations; 7–180-day lead bounds; 500-character requirements; maximum return mileage; and grade insurance caps. Its seed entry records units, basis, provenance and status. Collection also respects supplied renewal windows and closed dates. FUCQ-29 owns typed monthly points versus company/grade allowance and eligibility. Existing top-up keys remain unused; no contribution question, cash conversion or universal over-allowance rule is added. Critical unknowns yield `needs-confirmation` with a request-confirmation action.

Drafts append to shared `event_receipts`: consumer `manual:draft:<employeeId>`, event ID equal to `draftVersion`, command namespace `manual:<employeeId>:<commandId>`. Identical command replay returns its original outcome; changed replay conflicts. Expected draft/context versions and transactional employee/slot revision checks reject stale updates. Changing mode, old contract, scheme, slot, driver, contract option, collection date/location or brand invalidates selection, quote and eligible stock and increments `dependentGeneration`. Contact-only edits do not. Changed source, policy/configuration or slot context makes `readDraft` return `requiresRefresh`, cleared exposed references and a refresh-review view without rewriting history. FUCQ-66 owns actual claims, quote issuance, expiry and their revalidation.

`browseStock(context)` requires a current valid saved draft. It consumes all FUCQ-29 gate decisions with empty preferences, plus configured insurance and licence-at-collection checks. Eligible cars retain typed facts/terms and exact `orderUuid`, seven-digit `orderNumber` and `configurationId`, with `ranked: false`, null scores and `browsingReservesStock: false`. The recommendation display cap cannot truncate manual stock. Empty stock, no eligible stock and source outage differ. Browse creates no holds or stock writes. Repository stock uses existing `facts`, `release.availability` and `visibility: { companyIds, schemeIds, employeeIds: null|[] }`. Unknown visibility fails closed. Unreleased catalogue references, images, held/allocated cars and unknown facts cannot establish eligible availability. Exact stock has no VIN.

Compose `buildServer({ gcspIdentity, gcspClock, gcspManual, gcspAllowedOrigins })` explicitly for GET `/api/gcsp/manual/account`, GET/POST `/api/gcsp/manual/draft` and GET `/api/gcsp/manual/stock`. The shared employee/current-run/origin boundary and no-store responses apply. Queries and body authority overrides are invalid. Shared mappings provide 400 invalid, 403 forbidden, 404 absent draft, 409 conflict/stale and 503 unavailable; absent service composition stays unavailable.
