# GCSP UI and UX review, 9 November 2026 (FUCQ-248)

Story DEV-2026-11-09-GC-029. Reviewer: UI and UX SME agent (opus). Judged against `docs/gcsp/README.md`,
`demo-brief.md`, `experience-quality.md`, `build-rules.md`, `COMPONENTS.md` and the run sheet
`demo-walkthrough-2026-11-12.html` ("The 15 minutes"). No product code, copy, styles or fixtures were changed.

## Run 1: presenter controls and Paula

Scope: the presenter controls and entry page; Paula's employee journey (sign-in, Your cars, manual selection,
review, order, Resume); her car brief and buddy activity on the way to the offer; the offer and decision
(decline for Body style, change to Saloon, Tick, accept the second offer). Greg, the outbox, order progress
and help are run 2.

### Method

- Offline host (`GCSP_OFFLINE=1`, memory repository, ports 9290 to 9297), Chrome headless through Playwright,
  driven by `docs/gcsp/evidence/fucq-248/walk.mjs` (harness and checks) and `sections.mjs` (journeys):
  `WALK_SECTION=presenter|manual|offer|decision WALK_W=1280|375 WALK_PORT=… WALK_SCRATCH=… node walk.mjs`.
  States were reached with the presenter controls as the run sheet does: Reset (Decline then change, Main
  successful journey), Switch principal, Next release, Tick.
- The manual journey was walked in the scenario the host opens with (Clock/reset mechanics), as the FUCQ-70
  walkthrough does, because in Main successful journey the buddy's hold blocks every manual reservation
  (finding 10).
- Every screen at 1280x900 and 375x812, plus a 320 px reflow check on each. Screenshots are
  `docs/gcsp/evidence/fucq-248/<journey>-<width>-<nn>-<state>.png` (numbers run across one walk, so gaps
  between journeys are expected).
- Keyboard: a Tab walk on every screen records each focus stop, whether it shows a focus indicator and where
  focus sits after each action. Actions were taken by focusing the control and pressing Enter or Space.
- No automated checker (axe) is available offline, so the checks are scripted in `walk.mjs`: accessible
  names of focusable controls, target size (WCAG 2.5.8, 24 px; under 44 px counted), text contrast from
  computed colours against the nearest opaque background, horizontal overflow, heading outline, landmarks,
  live regions, images without `alt`, and em dashes in visible text. The screen reader check reads
  Playwright's ARIA snapshot (the accessibility tree) of each screen.

Scripted results that passed everywhere in run 1: every focusable control has an accessible name; no image
lacks `alt`; no text contrast failure in a settled state; no em dash on screen; no horizontal scroll at 375 or
320; no keyboard trap; every target is at least 24 px. Under 44 px (advisory, not AA): 11 controls on the car
brief, the "Car facts" toggles in the gallery, one control on setup and review.

### Findings, ranked by demo impact

Effort: S under a day, M one to three days, L more. Kit: as is, extend, or new.

**1. Internal codes, ids and raw timestamps on Paula's screens.** Paula, audience. Brief (1:30), offer
(5:00), gallery.
`paula-offer-1280-01-brief.png`, `paula-offer-1280-04-offer.png`, `paula-manual-1280-07-gallery-released.png`,
`paula-manual-1280-08-review.png`.
The brief's last card reads "allowance exceeded: allowance 1300, monthlyPoints 1400, unit points/month"; the
gallery gives reasons as "not available", "policy exclude", "allowance exceeded", "insurance grade limit";
the offer's current-car panel ends "Synthetic source: manual-records-v1. Observed 2026-11-05T09:00:00.000Z.";

Sport Touring". These are the first things a BMW audience reads as "not finished".

offered under your scheme", "Already reserved"), format dates with the kit's date formatter, title-case body

Kit: as is (copy and formatting in the views; a shared reason-label map). Effort M. **Fix.**

### What works and should stay

- The access gate: one field, one button, labelled, keyboard-ready (`presenter-1280-01-access-gate.png`).
- Error summary on setup: linked messages, focus on its heading, inline errors on each field.
- The brief's controls: real radio groups and labelled sliders, "I don't mind", "Not sure yet" options.
- Declarations gate Accept and Submit, with the hint "Tick each declaration to accept this offer".
- Resume returns to the review with focus on its h1, at both widths.
- The offer's current-car panel: a side-by-side comparison is the right idea; it needs aligned rows.
- Account: "Contract ends in 26 days, on 1 December 2026" is the right first fact; the outcome page
  ("Order received", reference, Finish) is clear and short.
- Responsive basics: no horizontal scroll at 375 or 320, no contrast failures, every control named.

### Screens and states not reached in run 1

- The buddy's release-to-offer transition on Buddy activity ("Found a car for you" appearing after Next
  release): Decline then change starts with the offer already held and Next release disabled ("No future
  release cue is configured"). In Main successful journey Next release did produce an offer ("View your
  offer" on Your cars) but the activity page was not captured in that scenario.
- Offer expired, No suitable match, Data unavailable and the uncertain-handoff scenarios.
- Opening the offer from the email: the walk used "View offer", which opens the same `/gcsp/offer` route the
  email links to. The email and outbox are run 2.
- Loading states: offline responses were too fast to capture; the FUCQ-70 walkthrough covers delayed stock
  and claim reads.
- Vehicle details dialog, print view, and the new-order journey (no fictional principal offers it in the
  default scenario).
- The static launcher picker (`index.html`) runs only under `npm run serve`; the hosted entry is the access
  gate, which was reviewed.
