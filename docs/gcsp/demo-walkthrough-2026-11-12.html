GCSP demo, Thursday 12 Nov

Prepared 9 Nov. Assumes every Jira ticket is built. This repo is public, so keep BMW-sensitive notes out of this file.

What we built

  • Paula's buddy: she sets preferences and must-haves once and lets it search. At each release it competes fairly for stock, holds a car and emails her. She can decline and change one thing, then gets a better second offer and accepts.
  • After she accepts: approval steps, a handover booking, documents and a separate return of her old car, each with a named owner.
  • Greg: sees what employees want before release. He plans a feasible order within policy limits, sends it to Production and replans when Production responds.
  • Comparison: today's release route against the buddy route, over a seeded cohort of fictional employees.
  • Underneath: every rule is a versioned policy value, every button changes real state, and external systems sit behind adapters.
  • How: since 30 Oct, the two of us directing coding agents: 3,411 commits, 244 Jira items and 3,531 automated tests.

"Is it real data and live endpoints?"

"The cars are real BMW data, collected from BMW's public configurator and stock locator and served from our own store. The people and scheme systems are synthetic, behind adapters shaped like the real interfaces. During the demo nothing calls BMW, GCSO, ISA or the mainframe. The only live call is the email it sends."

Checked in the code: the GCSP runtime's only outbound call is the Brevo email. The used-car side does use live retailer stock feeds.

Say it straight: over three seeded runs of 1,000 fictional employees, Covered before renewal was 14.8% on today's route and 11.8% on the buddy route.   • The learned model did worse than the baseline on the thin data, so none is active. Only mention it if asked.
