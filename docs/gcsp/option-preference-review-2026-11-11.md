## Interpreting the reviewed mappings

- **Well-specified:** `1` gives a normal distinct desirable, usable capability one point; higher numeric values give it more weight (`2` highly desirable, `3` exceptional/unicorn); `0` excludes it; `Bundle` requires resolving contents; `Unknown` waits for a description. Never add a bundle and the same included components. Use the highest applicable reviewed weight for a capability once. Heating and ventilation are separate functions; two heating tier codes are one function. Overlap groups are prompts for reconciliation, not a complete package bill of materials.
- **Other evidence:** `0` means no justified inference; `+1` supporting and `+2` strong evidence toward the high slider endpoint (positive evidence is capped at `+3`). Negative evidence points toward the low endpoint. Sportiness runs relaxed→sporty; comfort runs simple/functional→comfortable/luxurious; economy runs driving-first→economy-first; size runs compact→spacious. The review adds `EV` fuel support to the two charging-card codes. This is soft preference evidence for a known plug-in; it cannot change a car's authoritative powertrain or pass a fuel essential/charging gate.
- **Avoid invented benefits:** colour-only choices, administration, service plans, regulatory/baseline equipment and inactive preparation codes get no richer-spec credit. Premium materials and sporty styling have explicit subjective proposals for review. Wheel size alone never proves handling or poor ride. Aero-wheel and mild-hybrid economy evidence is weak, without a quantified saving. Sliding rear seats have weak proposed legroom evidence; towing/storage/folding seats do not imply a larger cabin.
- **Use existing importance:** option evidence belongs inside the relevant selected preference and its chosen importance. Use the strongest signal once per underlying capability, keep each dimension bounded, and avoid duplicate model/tag evidence. M Sport packages and their component markers share `sport-package`: their sportiness evidence must never stack. The hidden preference adds no visible control or user-input completion credit. The monotonic bounded calibration described below ensures more distinct desirable capabilities or a higher reviewed weight never reduces this score.
- **Keep prior rules:** essentials, points limits and the approved FUCQ-381 points-usage ranking remain in place. This proposal changes preference evidence; it does not bypass eligibility or replace the points-use tune.

## Backend implementation and calibration

`employee.recommendation.v1.optionEvidence` in the canonical policy seed contains the 202 mappings with a nonzero weight or preference signal. The companion reviewed matrix retains all 469 identifiers, including excluded codes. The runtime projection normalises `EV` to the existing `ev` enum, and records the reviewed workbook hash. No application service reads the workbook or draft source dictionaries.

The scorer sums the highest reviewed weight once per capability. On the shared fixed scale, equipment fit is `100 × totalWeight / (totalWeight + 8)`; eight desirability points therefore gives 50/100. The score is bounded and monotonic, without a candidate-relative maximum or a cutoff that makes all rich cars equal. It contributes at importance 100 alongside the selected preferences. Greg's existing multipliers continue to apply to the six visible preferences. These constants and bundle-overlap families live in policy and are revisable assumptions.

Slider signals contribute five observed-scale points per reviewed unit, capped at ±20 per dimension, before calculating fit to the user's selected value and importance. The opposite slider endpoint therefore has the opposite preference effect. Missing 0–62 time contributes the policy's neutral Sporty anchor of 50 at the user's chosen importance, with or without option evidence; selected sporty options can adjust that baseline. The missing fact remains explicit and does not disqualify the vehicle. Other missing slider facts require a nonzero selected option signal to use the neutral anchor; option evidence never invents a body/powertrain fact. A charging-card fuel signal can add five fit points for a known plug-in when EV is selected; it never rewrites the fuel fact. Missing exact specification is omitted rather than treated as a known basic car. Inactive preparation and subscription features require explicit activation evidence. Available dictionary codes receive no benefit.

Unresolved packages share conservative sport, technology/cockpit, comfort and connected-service overlap families. Each family contributes only the positive gap between its strongest fallback and the already selected constituent capabilities. This avoids package-plus-component inflation without inventing model-dependent package contents or claiming every S06U3 includes a HUD. S05ML, S033F and the M Sport roll-up markers share the sport capability. Suspension deletion explicitly supersedes S0704. Source labels and their model contexts remain preserved in the reviewed matrix.

The generated-stock composition carries the registry's actual selected factory/accessory codes into an `optionSelection` snapshot fact, labelled `selected-catalogue`. This is not a VIN-fitment claim. Completeness follows each admitted catalogue contract: schema 1 uses `complete` and `completeness`; schema 2 uses the separate factory/accessory-code flags. Complete selections can contribute the hidden equipment preference; partial selections only provide their known visible option signals. Gates, terms, points limits and anchored five-point ranking bands are unchanged. Older pinned policies without `optionEvidence` retain their original scoring. Existing callers without selected-option evidence receive no equipment contribution; the neutral missing-performance rule still applies under the updated policy. The hidden component never enters public preference registries, reasons/trade-offs or input-completion calculations.

No screen, control, layout or copy has changed. Backend presentation contracts are checked through the brief/editor and recommendation consumers; the collaborator's backend visual-review waiver applies. No backend deployment or restart is implied by a source commit. A fresh presenter run uses this policy once the updated backend is running; an already loaded server caches its seed.
