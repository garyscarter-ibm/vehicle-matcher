### Locking and idempotency (for the reviewer)
- **One occupying holder per car and per slot unit.** The V1 partial unique indexes on `custody_holds` decide it.
  - `Claim.claim` inserts inside a JDBC savepoint.
  - A 23505 there is recorded as an attempt `car-held` or `slot-occupied` (`Claim.conflictCode`), and the fallback continues.
- **Claim cursor.** `Claim.run` locks `allocation_runs` `FOR UPDATE`, revalidates the expected versions, then advances `next_index` by version CAS. The DB guard trigger keeps the plan immutable and the cursor forward-only.
- **Ids and replay.**
  - The allocation id is digest(type, sourceEventId).
  - The claim operation id is digest(run, allocation, employee, car).
  - Both replay through command receipts; a changed input gives `command-input-changed`.
  - Expiry commands are keyed by (run, now, clockVersion).
  - The trigger cursor (`AllocationTriggers`) moves by version CAS only after the allocation completes.
- **Uncertain outcomes.** `AllocationService.settle` inspects the receipt, retries once and never assumes.
- **Remote operations.**
  - `BuddyRemoteLifecycle.attemptWork`/`ackWork` lock `pending_operations` `FOR UPDATE` and CAS its version.
  - The provider call runs outside any transaction.
  - An unmatched, lost or not-found-from-execute acknowledgement stays pending/unknown, and the hold keeps occupying.
  - A release frees the car only on a confirmed acknowledgement at the expected stock version.
