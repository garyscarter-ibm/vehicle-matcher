  Found while fixing FUCQ-229 (dev/DEV-2026-11-08-GC-046), 9 Nov 2026.

  **What happens:** `server/gcsp/stock/index.js:110` rejects the whole stock pull if any one car's `availableUntil <= tick.now`. FUCQ-229 placed generated delivery weeks after the run's release horizon, so normal ticks are fine. But a 0-week lead time in policy, or a presenter clock jump (`to`) past a delivery week, still wedges every later pull with "The source availability interval has ended." This happened under the old seed too.

  **Proposed fix:** the generated provider leaves out cars whose availability has ended before it builds a pull, and records them as expired. The port's strict validation stays unchanged.

  **Also for the owner (question, not a defect):** generated runs' three Next releases release only window-0 cars, about 60 of 300 to 400 generated. Is that intended?

  Severity: Medium. It only happens with generated stock plus a time jump or a 0-week lead.
