  diagnosis: Reproduced locally at gcsp-poc commit 0b0ed5349eda with independent memory runtime, actual HTTPS presenter/employee routes, actual services and generated inventory. Six scenario branches/eight declines completed. Fuel changes persist as new confirmed brief versions. Allocation removes the exact-car exclusion whenever semantic confirmed brief content differs from the brief recorded by a decline (allocation/index.js lines 183–189). This is current documented untilRelevantConfirmedBriefChange behavior, not a failed save. No check requires improvement against the decline reason, satisfaction of the chosen fuel, or a minimum fit score: gateStatus eligible is sufficient to contend. Main journey starts with body estate (importance 80), no fuel preference, and charging not-ready. Fuel follow-up adds importance 50. EV/PHEV entries are blocked by charging-unavailable; combustion entries stay eligible even when they score 2/100 against the revised brief. Seeded tie-breaking can choose the exact previously declined car again.

  Observed exact repeat: X1 23d in Black Sapphire, order 2bb358cc-85f9-4870-a185-3bc15e456a8a, is reoffered after adding diesel and again after changing diesel to EV. The latter new confirmed brief retains EV while the offered diesel scores 2. A saloon-change control also reoffers the exact X1 M35i when eligible saloons are absent. Unchanged decline excludes its exact car correctly. Home-charging control changes from a plug-in hybrid X1 25e to an electric iX2 30. Petrol preference later changes the looping diesel offer to a 320i Touring. Thus "every release" is not universal; exact repeats are demonstrated under concrete inputs.

  Affected writers/readers: BriefService saves the answer and brief-change event; allocation consumes saved briefs and decisions, re-evaluates stock and sends a new hold; follow-up reads the actual new offer. No stale-screen or lost-save cause was found in this reproduction. No production rows, policy or business code changed. Next check: agree whether the rejected exact car stays excluded for the renewal or can re-enter only on reason-specific evidence of improvement; preserve legitimate soft-preference semantics.
