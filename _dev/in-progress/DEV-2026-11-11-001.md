goal: As Paula, I want to see the offered car, meaningful differences and remaining decision time together, so I can decide without searching through a narrow card or declarations.
acceptance:
- [ ] The pending offer uses the available full content width, consistent with Your cars. It shows vehicle identity, pinned points terms and supplied facts. Paint and upholstery show readable prose plus supported colour swatches; unknown finish colours remain neutral rather than invented. Omit Scheme: Choices from vehicle details and omit an order number from an unaccepted offer. A real post-acceptance order number remains in its existing outcome/progress context.
- [ ] A prominent countdown shows hours:minutes: a newly generated two-hour offer starts at 2:00, proceeds through 1:59 and 0:01 to 0:00. Reuse the server expiry and DemoClock authority; reload/second browser and presenter clock advances do not reset it. Zero triggers authoritative reread and the actual offer state, never a client-invented expiry. Unavailable timing has a concise state and cannot enable a stale decision.
- [ ] Keep large-number differences grouped as Benefits and Tradeoffs, with Benefits slightly accentuated. At desktop, equal thirds align highlight boundaries with all comparison columns; Benefits occupies two columns and Tradeoffs one. Five or more benefits wrap to additional two-card rows; no silent three-benefit cap, overlap, clipped text, misleading merged empty cell or inflated tradeoff. At narrow widths the groups stack with readable cards. Empty/unknown groups do not invent a benefit.
- [ ] The comparison names current and offered configurations and highlights only the winning cell for each grounded, directionally comparable row, whether current or offer. Ties, unknowns and differences without an agreed preference stay neutral. No permanently highlighted offer column or duplicate comparison caption. FUCQ-337 supplies meaningful exact-option comparison scope; do not duplicate or rewrite it.
- [ ] Accept offer is the primary action, Decline offer the next most prominent, visible above the fold at representative desktop and 375px/320px narrow sizes before the gallery. They open the separately delivered decision drawers. Keep pending, accepted, declined, expired, earlier-run, not-found and unavailable states truthful and preserve existing navigation/follow-up.
- [ ] Reuse/extend the kit with catalogue/gallery updates and ratchet compliance. Keep custom-element embedding, CSP without inline scripts/handlers/eval, keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced motion and long-content layouts.
