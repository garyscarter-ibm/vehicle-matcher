goal:
  Found while building FUCQ-135.

  `employee.recommendation.v1.termValiditySeconds` pins quote terms for 3,600 s, but the buddy hold lasts 7,200 s. If an employee answers more than an hour after the offer, they can't accept it, even though the car is still held for them. This is also visible in the live demo when the presenter advances the clock past an hour.

  **Expected:** a held offer can be accepted for the whole hold. Either the quote lasts at least as long as the hold, or the offer's expiry is the earlier of the two and the countdown says so. Both are policy assumptions, so record the chosen values and why in the canonical seed.
