acceptance:
- [ ] **Order progress:** on her account (FUCQ-67) and on the offer page (FUCQ-105) once selected, Paula sees the selected car (with `mediaWell` and its 7-digit `orderNumber`) and the approval stages from FUCQ-111 in the target's names. - [ ] **Handover appointment:** show FUCQ-114's requested, proposed-change, pending and scheduled states, never calling a requested slot booked. Paula can request once sent to ordering and accept or refuse a proposed change. Completion/no-show UI moves to FUCQ-169.
- [ ] **Documents and readiness:** the items from FUCQ-116 show as a task list, with Paula's own tasks first and their actions, then items others own with their owner and due time. Overdue and failed items are distinct.
- [ ] **Old-car return:** for a replacement order, show FUCQ-118's distinct requested/scheduled return plan separately from the new-car appointment. Her existing car remains current throughout planning.
- [ ] **Every state rendered:** waiting for HR/sales-manager approval; approval declined with its next step and help entry; sent to ordering with no slot; requested/scheduled and proposed-change/pending appointments; documents waiting for Paula, in progress, overdue or failed; ready for handover; separately requested/scheduled old-car return; unsupported provider write shown as not done; loading and source failure. Reload/reopening read the server's actual state. Completed/no-show/missed-return presentation moves to FUCQ-169.
- [ ] **Copy:** labels use the target's stage names and say plainly who is acting. The demo signing stand-in is labelled synthetic. No em dashes.
- [ ] **Shared kit:** the views are built from the shared kit per `COMPONENTS.md`. New or extended components go into the kit, the catalogue and the gallery in the same commit. The ratchet baseline only falls.
- [ ] **Embeddable (Q18):** the block mounts as a custom element, with no inline scripts, inline handlers or `eval`. Keyboard access, visible focus and status announcements throughout.
