  diagnosis (story-lead, 9 Nov 2026, code basis dev/DEV-2026-11-09-GC-044 b26bdb85 on gcsp-poc 8a6a3153, offline
  fixtures): presentation-only fault; the engine is right. In the demo brief only Body style is set (Estate,
  importance 80); the other preferences have importance 0 and are skipped. `scorePreferences`
  (server/scenarios/employee/index.js) scores body as a categorical taste match, so an estate scores 100 and a
  saloon or hatchback 0, and the car score is that one component. The server ranks eligible cars by score and
  files a preference-fit at 100 under reasons, below 100 under tradeOffs (the brief already heads them
  "Trade-offs"). The faults are in the views: the brief passes `matchCard` its default `showScore`, so every
  eligible card shows `${score}%` ("0%"), and the shared reason copy prints preference-fit as "Body style: 0% fit."
  Unset sliders read "Not supplied" because `formatSliderValue` and the single slider in
  blocks/vehicle-matcher/modes/question-ui.js use that text for an unknown value. Readers: brief cards, and the
  offer card through offer-projection `decisionView` (no badge there, showScore false). No stored data affected.
  Repair: keep scoring and ranking. Zero-fit cars stay listed (they are eligible and she may still choose them),
  labelled honestly: no percentage badge; a "Best match" badge only on the eligible cars that share the top score
  when that score is above 0 and not every eligible car shares it; preference-fit reasons and trade-offs in words
  ("Estate, the body style you prefer.", "Saloon, not the estate you prefer.", "Less sporty than you prefer.");
  unset sliders read "Not set" on the brief through a kit option, other screens unchanged.

  docs/gcsp/ux-review-2026-11-09.md.
