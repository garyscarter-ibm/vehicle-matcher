Preserve image provenance, retained asset/digest/viewpoint and valid association/fallback basis; never relabel one front image as rear/interior or infer exact physical-car appearance.
- [ ] Extend the existing retained-image registry/provider and vehicle/offer projection to carry an ordered gallery. Prefer supported exact configuration associations, then correctly matched model/body/paint basis as available. Historical pinned data and other existing single-image consumers remain compatible; public payloads expose no keys or private/signed upstream URLs.
- [ ] Selecting a small gallery thumbnail replaces the featured image in place and updates the selected state/alt text. It does not open a new image/window. The magnifier opens the currently selected image in a larger viewer.
- [ ] The larger viewer has the selected image, a small horizontally scrollable thumbnail strip at the bottom, previous/next controls, and left/right arrow-key navigation including first/last wrap. Selection stays synchronized between strip, counter, main thumbnail and featured image. Close/Escape restores focus to the magnifier; focus stays within the open viewer and active thumbnails remain reachable/visible.
- [ ] Missing, broken, loading, empty and single-image data produce useful readable fallbacks without broken image icons, false minimum-coverage claims or phantom controls. Minimum seeded three-view coverage remains an acceptance obligation for supported vehicles.
- [ ] Whole-car images, controls and gallery fit desktop, 375px and 320px; touch targets, keyboard focus, long labels and reduced motion are usable. No on-screen representative-image/demo disclaimer or superfluous source/navigation links. Custom-element/CSP and shared-kit rules hold.
