decided:

  * diagnosis: reproduction: verified from the FUCQ-149 walk evidence above (results-phone.json, results-desktop.json; trace /account +5160 ms). Cause: each journey mount (blocks/gcsp/manual-setup/journey.js mount) tears down the old view, then mountEmployee shows only a role="status" paragraph "Loading your account and cars." with no heading until its per-mount account read resolves; the mount focus helper targets only "main h1, main h2", so focus stays on BODY for the account read's latency. Affected readers: keyboard and screen-reader users on every journey mount transition (account, checking, setup, gallery, review, summary); no data, claim or cache effect. Repair: let the same helper fall back to the main status region while no heading exists and move on to the heading when it arrives, without stealing from a user-moved focus.
