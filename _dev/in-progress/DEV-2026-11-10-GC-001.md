goal:
  Gary, 9 Nov 2026: "remove any disclaimers or text related to this being a demo, synthetic data, demo data, example names etc. I'll cover it verbally, it makes the app look unauthentic and cluttered."

  This changes agreed direction. demo-brief.md (Build principles) asks for "realistic, explicitly identified scenario data", and FUCQ-279 added a scenario footer to each screen to meet it. Under this Story, scenario data is identified by the presenter and in the docs, not on screen. Provenance stays in the data, API, logs and docs.
acceptance:
- [ ] No screen Paula or Greg sees, and no email, says or implies the product is a demo or that its data is synthetic, fictional, made up or an example. That covers headings, footers, notes, badges, name suffixes, status lines and error copy. Examples found on gcsp-poc:
  the kit synthetic-note footers (blocks/gcsp/kit/synthetic-note.js, added by FUCQ-277/279);
  "Paula (synthetic)", "Scheme employee (fictional)" and `${id} (fictional)`;
  "Fictional demonstration terms" and "I accept these fictional scheme terms";
  "Choices, synthetic demonstration";
  "Synthetic production controls";
  "Synthetic-trained model" and "Synthetic identity";
  "Demo time at last check";
  "This request belongs to an earlier demonstration run.", "The demonstration run changed." and "Reading the current demonstration run.";
  the email preview's "This is a captured demonstration message for a fictional employee. No email has been sent.";
  car names carrying "Synthetic" or "Fictional" (this also covers FUCQ-315).
- [ ] People's names read as real names. Shown email addresses read as plausible, and stay unroutable or limited to the configured demo inbox, so no mail can reach a real person.
- [ ] Copy that remains still makes sense. For example, a stale-page message says the page is out of date and to reload, without mentioning a "run".
- [ ] Greg's comparison keeps its assumptions and limitations list, because that is the analysis itself. Reword items that only say "fictional" so they state the assumption (for example "People, cars and behaviour are modelled, not BMW records" becomes an assumption line, not a disclaimer). Gary to confirm the wording at review.
- [ ] Tests that pinned the removed strings are updated, and a new test fails if any of the listed words come back into painted Paula or Greg copy.
