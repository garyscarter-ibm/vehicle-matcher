decided (story-lead, 10 November 2026):
- Route lives in `server/gcsp/presenter-releases/http.js` and is registered in `server/index.js` beside the outbox and presenter-controls routes (that is where those are registered; `host.js` composes the runtime only).
- "onScheme" = every stock row in the run with `state: released`, `available: true`, `held !== true`, `allocated !== true`, across all releases. This is the predicate Greg's demand and manual browse already use.
- "Latest release" = the completed `stock-release` receipt with the latest `releasedAt`, ties broken by tranche id. `release` also carries `trancheId` (an added field; nothing renamed), because generated stock can release two tranches under one window id.
- `colourName` is the row's `colour` fact (generated stock), else the paint named in its catalogue picture's configuration through `stock.optionLabels.v1` (fixed demo stock), else `null`. The G20 Saloon catalogue picture names no paint, so on demo stock the saloon's colour is `null` and is not counted.
- `modelLine` is the `policy.displayLabels.v1` line name (for example "BMW 1 Series Sports Hatch"); `modelName` is the recorded display name without its "in <colour>" suffix.
- A runtime with no stock release service answers `503 unavailable` (`retryable: true`).
