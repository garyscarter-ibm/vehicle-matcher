  **Steps to reproduce:** as Greg, close any renewal-risk case. Then open the case queue and the case views.

  **Expected:** a closed or resolved case has no next action or future due date, or shows them as finished.

  **Actual:** `update()` in `server/gcsp/cases/index.js` (around lines 303 to 307) requires `nextAction` and a `dueAt` later than the demo clock's now on every update, including a close. As a result, the queue's "Next action due" column and both case views show a future due date on a closed case.

  **Likely fix:** for a status change to resolved or closed, make `nextAction` and `dueAt` optional, or record them as finished. Then show nothing, or "Closed", in the queue and the case views. Add a regression test in gcsp-renewal-case and gcsp-case-views.

  **Impact:** misleading copy on post-demo case routes. Not in the default demo.
