  Deferred from the 10 November desktop UX revamp (Paula findings 11 and 12, `docs/gcsp/ux-revamp-review-2026-11-10-paula.md`). This is layout work that keeps current behaviour, deferred to keep the revamp focused on the run sheet's main journey.

  **Problem:** Released cars puts three count and history lines above the cars. Blocked cards still show their actions, and ineligibility is said three ways. Review, summary and Order received repeat the order up to four times, and they show or edit contact details on five screens.

  **Proposal:**

  * Remove the noise lines and collapse the ineligible cars.
  * Add a blocked-card variant with no actions.
  * Render review, summary and outcome as rows, not cards.
  * Capture contact details once in setup, then show them as a single "Your details" row with Change.
  * Give each step one exit.
