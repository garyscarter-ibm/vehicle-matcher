  Found by the independent security review of FUCQ-455 (verdict PASS), merged into gcsp-poc-dev at e0bfa98c. All three findings are Low severity. Under the standing defect policy they are captured here for independent pickup; none blocks FUCQ-455.

  1. **Duplicate scope gives 500.**

      * Where: `services/gcsp-api/src/main/java/gcsp/api/identity/OidcIdentity.java:197`.
      * `Set.of(s.trim().split("\s+"))` throws `IllegalArgumentException` when the `scope` claim repeats a value, and `authorize()` only catches `MalformedClaimException`.
      * Repro: a validly signed token with `scope="openid gcsp-api gcsp-api"` returns 500 `unexpected` plus an error-level stack trace, instead of 200/403.
      * Fix: collect into a HashSet, and add a duplicate-scope test.

  2. **Unknown** `kid` refetch throttle.

      * Where: `OidcIdentity.java:74-78`.
      * A token with an unknown `kid` makes jose4j refetch the JWKS before the signature check. The default limit is one fetch per 300 ms, under a lock.
      * Effect: unauthenticated callers with random `kid`s drive about 3 IdP requests a second, and requests queue behind the 2 s timeout. While the IdP is down, a forged token gets 503 `identity-keys-unavailable` instead of 401.
      * Fix: `jwks.setRefreshReprieveThreshold(...)`, around 30–60 s.

  3. **No key-rotation test.**

      * Where: `src/test/java/gcsp/api/OidcContextTest.java:183`.
      * No test covers key rotation (a new `kid` published after startup) or the unknown-`kid` 401, yet `docs/gcsp/oidc-test-provider-2026-11-13.md:34` claims the refetch behaviour.
      * Fix: have `JwksServerResource` publish a second key mid-test, then assert 200 for the new key and 401 for an unknown `kid`.
