goal:
  Found while building FUCQ-135 (merged f7f990f4).

  At every contender step, FUCQ-96's `allocate` re-saves and deep-copies the whole contender plan, including full quotes. The cost therefore grows quadratically with the number of participant and eligible-car pairs.

  **Measured:**

  | Participant and car pairs | Time |
  | --- | --- |
  | 24 | 0.6 s |
  | 46 | 1.9 s |
  | 84 | 6.1 s |

  With 40 employees and 100-car releases, the run did not finish within 170 s.

  **Impact:**

  * A 1,000-employee buddy seed is not practical. This blocks FUCQ-136's measures at the demo's cohort size.
  * A presenter tick slows down as the live pool grows.

  **Expected:** allocation time stays roughly linear. The recommended fix keeps behaviour the same: store the plan once, and advance a small cursor or assignment receipt instead of re-saving the whole plan each step. The tie-break, idempotency and the existing FUCQ-96, FUCQ-100 and critical-path tests must stay unchanged.
