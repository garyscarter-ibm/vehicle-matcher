  **Requested by Gary Carter on 8 Nov 2026. Priority: Should.**

  When no released car meets Paula's brief, her Buddy activity says only "Still searching. No match yet. … 0 suitable". It doesn't say why. The FUCQ-127 no-match replay (merged b6922323) shows the problem: every car fails on `essential-unmet:seats`, but the screen doesn't say so.

  **Acceptance:**

  * When the last check found no suitable car, Buddy activity names the essential that ruled the cars out. The name comes from the allocation decisions' reason codes, for example "No released car has 9 seats". With more than one excluding essential, show the top one or two, in plain language.
  * Offer two next steps: "Keep searching", which leaves the brief as it is, and "Review your brief", which opens the brief editor. Neither changes the brief by itself.
  * Only Paula's own decisions are used. Nothing about other employees is shown.
  * It works in the FUCQ-127 replay and in the main journey. There's a render test for each wording.
  * Experience quality: shared kit components, keyboard access and a status announcement. No em dashes in on-screen copy.

  **Areas:** `server/gcsp/brief/activity.js` (now reading per-employee evaluation rows since FUCQ-209) and the activity view in `blocks/gcsp/`.
