  Found by the dispatcher's full npm test on gcsp-poc, 8 Nov 2026, 23:40. It was introduced by the FUCQ-78 merge a0892124: the test passes at b8b3e045 and fails from a0892124.

  **Failing test:** `server/test/gcsp-run-stock.test.js`, "eligibility keys come from policy…". At line 111 the second presenter tick returns `unavailable`. The stock-release handler outcome is `invalid-stock-pull`, availability: "The source availability interval has ended."

  **Cause (diagnosed):**

  * `server/gcsp/stock/generated-provider.js:22` sets each car's `availability.availableUntil` to its `confirmedDeliveryWeek`.
  * FUCQ-78 replaced the assumed `stock.aggregate.*` entries with live-sampled ones, whose lead-time and delivery-date spreads are anchored to the real sample date, 8 Nov.
  * So some generated cars now have a delivery week at or before the demo clock's second release (2026-11-09T11:30Z).
  * `server/gcsp/stock/index.js:110` then rejects the whole pull when any one car's `availableUntil <= tick.now`.

  **Impact:** only presenter runs that choose generated stock. The default demo stock, critical path and scenario replays pass. But a presenter who picks generated stock gets a failed Next release from the second tick on.

  **Expected:** generated stock stays releasable for the whole demo run. Either generated delivery dates are placed relative to the run's own release windows, not the sample date, or a car whose availability has ended is left out of a pull rather than voiding it. Fix it at the generator or provider, and keep the stock port's validation strict.
