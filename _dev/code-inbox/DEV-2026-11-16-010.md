  Found by the concurrency review of FUCQ-421 (merged into gcsp-poc-dev at 0431d674). Severity: Medium.

  **Where:** `server/gcsp/migrations/0010_repository_revisions.sql`, lines 42–56. The trigger table list is hardcoded.

  **Problem:** the legacy fingerprint followed `FOUNDATION_TABLES` in `schema.js` automatically. The revision check now depends on this copied list instead.

  **Scenario:** a later story adds table `foo` to `schema.js` but doesn't add its triggers. Instance A inserts into `foo`, and no revision moves. Instance B then serves its cached, stale `foo` rows as fresh, with no error.

  **Suggested fix:** add a native test, or a check in `open()`, that the tables carrying `revision_*` triggers in `pg_trigger` equal `FOUNDATION_TABLES`.

  **Not affected today:** all 22 current tables carry their 4 triggers (88 in total), as verified on PostgreSQL 17.
