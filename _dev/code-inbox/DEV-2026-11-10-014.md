  Raised from Gary's review, 10 November 2026. Suggested MoSCoW: **Should**.

  **Problem:** after Paula starts the manual "Choose a replacement" route and stops, Your cars shows a "Replacement journey saved" card ("Your starting context is saved. No car has been selected or ordered.") with only **Resume saved journey**. She has no way to cancel it. The server has read and save for the draft but no delete, so only a presenter reset or an order clears it. The wording is internal and the full-width primary button makes it look like something she must act on.

  **Intent:**

  * Paula can discard a saved journey from that card, with a confirmation. Afterwards the card is gone and the account shows her normal choices.
  * Plain copy, for example: "You started choosing a replacement for your 3 Series Touring", with **Carry on** and **Discard**.
  * A held car or an order is never affected by a discard.
