goal: As Paula, I want to review the required declarations or give a fixed decline reason in a focused fly-out, so that I can confirm my decision while retaining the vehicle context.
acceptance:
- [ ] Prominent Accept offer and Decline offer actions open the corresponding single side drawer, visually consistent with Your cars View record. The drawer identifies the same offered configuration and pinned points terms. Close, Escape or Keep reviewing/Keep this offer leaves the pending offer undecided and restores focus; background interaction is contained while open.
- [ ] The acceptance drawer supersedes the old inline declaration/terms decision area. It includes the authoritative contact/return context and all three incumbent declarations (scheme terms; nominated driver/licence; old contract/expected return mileage), with working relevant document links. Show supplied recorded versus expected mileage correctly; unknown or invalid required details are explicit. The final Accept is disabled until every required declaration and existing handoff prerequisite is valid. Preserve termsDigest and declaration IDs from the server; no pre-ticked or fabricated confirmations.
- [ ] The decline drawer supersedes the old inline decline form and has required fixed choices from PolicyService offer.declineReasons.v1, with current codes/labels. It has no free-text input, no free-text payload and no locally invented choices. Show initial/unselected, selected, reason-required validation, loading and unavailable-retry states. Confirm decline submits only a valid current reason code; Keep this offer cancels it.
- [ ] Reuse existing authoritative decision endpoints/controller and access/run/employee context. A first click opens review without submitting. Final decisions preserve server revalidation, pinned terms, command-id idempotency, pending feedback and duplicate-click suppression; stale/conflicting/expired/unavailable responses show the actual state without a false success.
- [ ] Accepted outcome retains existing scheme-check/order-progress navigation; declined outcome enters the existing fixed follow-up journey. Decline remains distinct from changing the brief and after-use feedback; no free-text consuming workflow is added.
- [ ] Drawer header/scrolling body/footer and final action remain usable at desktop, 375px and 320px with long context, all declarations and validation. Keyboard-only focus trap/return, error announcement/field focus, document navigation, readable controls, custom-element/CSP and kit ratchet requirements hold.
