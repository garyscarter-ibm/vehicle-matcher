acceptance:
- [ ] Provide a stateless read-only employee application consumer and explicitly bound /api/gcsp recommendation contract, disabled or rejected when no permitted scenario/context provider is configured.
- [ ] The server resolves a supported fictional employee/scheme context, policy and stock source through injected adapters; browser-supplied employee IDs, grade, quotes, availability or retail-profile flags cannot widen permission or bypass the policy.
- [ ] Consume a versioned, explicitly selected October 2024 matrix fixture from the agreed baseline; missing points/driver/stock details use labelled synthetic assumptions. Unknown scheme/grade/context fails explicitly, without BMW/retail fallback.
- [ ] Evaluate eligibility, confirmed essentials including carrying/equipment/charging, the points allowance (the employee's company/grade allowance against the car's monthly points cost) and required timing before preference scoring and display caps. A cash top-up stays an unused keyed assumption. Return eligible, ineligible or needs-confirmation outcomes with grounded blockers/unknowns; an essential-unknown car is not asserted suitable.
- [ ] Use shared meaningful dimension curves with separately configured employee preferences, importance and indifference. Stated facts/essentials survive game inference and strategic steering; contribution/points are never fake retail prices or budgets.
- [ ] Return exact decisions with brief/facts/policy/terms/stock versions, reasons and trade-offs; zero viable stock and unavailable data are distinct. Equivalent synthetic/real-shaped provider contract fixtures and changed inputs produce computed outcomes rather than named winners.
- [ ] Retail contracts and six-brand/five-mode behaviour remain compatible; no route saves a brief, claims a car, creates an offer or sends a notification.
