  This is deterministic test-fixture aging, not a product ordering regression. At the 25 November account date, the packaged Paula collection window ends on 1 December while the canonical seven-day minimum lead makes 2 December the earliest admissible date. There is no valid date, so the service's `ordering-closed` result is correct and must remain.

  The existing `prepareCollectionWindows` helper widens only the in-run synthetic account inputs, derives dates from the actual account's as-of date and canonical bounds, and asserts that drafts, claims, holds and orders have not started. Call it once during each initial setup, then read the account again. Do not call it from reusable `prepare(who)`: the remote journey prepares Jordan after Paula's order.

  FUCQ-587 repaired the earlier fixed 30 November inputs that violated the seven-day minimum on 24 November. It deliberately did not extend packaged windows. This Bug captures the separate next-day expiry; do not reopen FUCQ-587 or FUCQ-583.
