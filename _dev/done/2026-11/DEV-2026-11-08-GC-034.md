- diagnosis (confirmed): most remaining attempts are wasted. Allocation evaluates a plug-in car using the employee's stored charging fact when the brief has no charging answer. The FUCQ-100 hold service's `evaluateCandidate` always takes charging from the brief (unknown when absent), so it refuses every such car with `candidate-not-eligible` (`charging-unconfirmed`). That was 27 of 49 attempts at 60 employees and 399 of 511 at 150. Each refusal is a full hold transaction. The pattern is the same on base.
- decision for Gary (High, blocks the 1,000-employee target): should allocation judge charging the same way as the hold service? Recommendation: yes. A scratch-only change in `allocation/index.js` evaluates gates with the brief-derived charging fact and keeps the stored fact in the quote. It produces the identical buddy outcome digest at 60, 150 and 300 employees, and removes the wasted attempts. Visible effect: for an employee with no charging answer, Buddy's evaluation records plug-in cars as needing confirmation instead of suitable. Buddy activity's `suitableCars` count can fall, and follow-up can say no-match instead of waiting, which is truthful, since the server would never hold those cars. The other option is for the hold service to accept the stored fact, which changes offers and comparison results.
- remaining after the decision (Medium, separate): at 1,000 employees, 3.9 GB RSS and 15.1 min. The remaining cost is the evaluator's per-employee pool clone and validation, the hold service listing all released stock on every claim, and repository scans that grow with row count.
