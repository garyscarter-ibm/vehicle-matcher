goal:
  Found while building FUCQ-135 (merged f7f990f4).

  At every contender step, FUCQ-96's `allocate` re-saves and deep-copies the whole contender plan, including full quotes. The cost therefore grows quadratically with the number of participant and eligible-car pairs.

  **Measured:**

  | Participant and car pairs | Time |
  | --- | --- |
  | 24 | 0.6 s |
  | 46 | 1.9 s |
  | 84 | 6.1 s |

  With 40 employees and 100-car releases, the run did not finish within 170 s.

  **Impact:**

  * A 1,000-employee buddy seed is not practical. This blocks FUCQ-136's measures at the demo's cohort size.
  * A presenter tick slows down as the live pool grows.

  **Expected:** allocation time stays roughly linear. The recommended fix keeps behaviour the same: store the plan once, and advance a small cursor or assignment receipt instead of re-saving the whole plan each step. The tie-break, idempotency and the existing FUCQ-96, FUCQ-100 and critical-path tests must stay unchanged.

## Result

- **Diagnosis (confirmed):** `allocate` in `server/gcsp/allocation/index.js` wrote the whole pending plan receipt (every contender with its full quote) back through `compareAndSwap` after each contender step, so every step deep-copied and re-persisted the entire plan. Cost was steps x plan size, hence quadratic in participant x eligible-car pairs.
- **Repair:** the plan receipt is written once at prepare and only completed at the end. Progress (`next`, `assignments`, `attempts`) lives in a small `buddy-allocation-cursor` receipt, advanced only when a claim is attempted; skipped contenders need no write because a retry skips them again from the same assignments. Completion closes the cursor and completes the plan in one transaction. A plan prepared before this change migrates its progress to a cursor once. Tie-break, idempotency, the completed outcome shape and the `buddy-allocation` receipt readers (`offers/followup.js`, `brief/activity.js`) are unchanged.
- **Regression test:** `server/test/gcsp-allocation.test.js` "FUCQ-205: the plan is written once..." (12 cars, 30+ contenders, 3 attempts). Before the fix it failed: the plan receipt reached version 38 (expected 2) and the test took about 4 s. After: version 2, at most 4 + attempts allocation commands, 0.96 s.
- **Tests (scoped):** allocation, allocation-runtime, allocation-triggers, identity-allocation, vin-allocation, all buddy-* (FUCQ-100 hold and expiry), critical-path, case-release-http, comparison-current/buddy/generator, current-car-comparison, second-offer-*, offer-view-followup-*, all activity-* tests: 383 run, 382 pass, 0 fail, 1 skipped (pre-existing skip). Full `npm test` not run (dispatcher runs it). PostgreSQL unrun (memory repository only).
- **Not measured here:** the 1,000-employee buddy seed (FUCQ-136 will measure it at scale).
